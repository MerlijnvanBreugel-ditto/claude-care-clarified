# The current state of the brand: your canvas

Read this as a map of a brand mid-construction. Very little is locked, on purpose. The few settled things are the ones we already say in public: the mission, the way we pitch Ditto, the founder story. Almost everything about expression, personality, and the visual system is still open. **That is the job to be done, and it's why you're here.**

## What's solid

- **The "why."** No one should go through healthcare in the dark. Felt, consistent, public.
- **Mission:** "We clarify and connect care journeys for you and your loved ones."
- **Tagline:** "Care. Clarified." in active use, with "Clarity when it matters most" as a working payoff line.
- **The public narrative:** the consumer layer European healthcare has been missing; right info, right format, right time; the grandmother origin; the traction story.
- **The brand ambition** (a strong north star): healthcare brands pick a side, clinical-and-trusted or warm-and-shallow. Ditto refuses the choice. Human without being soft, innovative without losing credibility, personal enough to sit with someone during a hard diagnosis, universal enough to cross cultures and generations. It must earn trust in living rooms, waiting rooms, and boardrooms alike. Evolution over revolution.

## What's a working draft

- **Values, the four convictions:** the patient unlocks everything; health is never solo; simplicity is the feature; earn trust, don't extract it. Useful, wording open.
- **Brand promise:** after every healthcare moment, you and your people understand what happened, what it means, and what to do next. On-message, not yet ratified.
- **Voice and tone:** the most developed part. Clarity with intelligence (clear is not the same as simple), human and warm, confident not corporate, trustworthy by default. EN + NL, adapt don't translate. Still a documented draft, not a locked standard.

## What's genuinely open (the real work)

- **An explicit, user-facing positioning statement.** The public version is an investor narrative. We don't have one crisp line a normal user would recognize.
- **Personality.** Undefined beyond "positive, uplifting, present." The task: move from generic warmth to something only Ditto would say, and decide whether the register should be calmer than "uplifting" for people who've just been diagnosed.
- **The one ownable visual idea.** The flexible "o" is the leading candidate. Headspace has its illustrations; what's ours?
- **The visual system.** Grown organically, not where we want it. We're moving toward photography on web, video is gaining traction on social, and illustration's role in the app is undecided. Colour and type aren't formalised.
- **Does the app feel like Ditto?** Never audited. Nobody has asked whether the product itself carries the brand.

## The honest summary

The *why* is solid. *How we show up* is wide open. A crisp user-facing positioning, a defined personality, and a visual system you actually believe in are the live construction sites. There's no finalised brandbook for the current direction yet. Producing one is likely your first big deliverable.

This is unusual. Most brand leads inherit a system and maintain it. You're inheriting a mission and a blank-ish canvas. Treat that as the opportunity it is.
