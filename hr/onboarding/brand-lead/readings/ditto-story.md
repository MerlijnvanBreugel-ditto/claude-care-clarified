# The Ditto story

## Where it started

Riet, my grandmother, would say it plainly: *"I remember less than half of what the doctor said."* So she brought one of her kids to every hospital visit, not because she couldn't cope, but because the system handed her information in the worst possible way and then expected her to carry it home alone.

I'm Merlijn, her grandson, and one of Ditto's founders. After every visit, the real work began: explaining what happened to the rest of the family, working out what it actually meant, figuring out what to do next. That work happens in the kitchen, in the group chat, in the quiet moment re-reading what the doctor said. Healthcare is built for providers. It leaves people passive and ill-understood. It forces us to be *patient*.

We didn't think that should be the case.

## What Ditto is

Ditto is building the consumer layer European healthcare has been missing: the right information, in the right format, at the right time, for you and the people who care about you.

Today that's an AI-powered app that clarifies every healthcare encounter and connects you with the people around you through the **Care Circle**. You capture a conversation, we explain it in language a human understands, and you can share it with your partner, your kids, whoever needs to know. The same information, translated differently for each person who needs it.

**Capture → Clarify → Connect.** That's the spine.

## Where we're going

What you see today is the start. We're building toward a platform that lets you navigate healthcare with convenience and clarity, for yourself and with the people you love. The shorthand we use: *the Polarsteps for Health, and yes, at least European.*

The tagline is **"Care. Clarified."** The payoff line we reach for is *"Clarity when it matters most."*

That's the company you've joined. The mission is settled and felt. How we *show up* in the world, that's the part that's wide open, and that's your job.
