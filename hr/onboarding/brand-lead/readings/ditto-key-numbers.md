# Ditto by the numbers

The short version. Not a dashboard, just the figures that explain where we are, where we're going, and why brand is on the critical path. (Internal. Roughly current as of Q2 2026.)

## Traction

- **~80,000 people** have used Ditto to understand their care.
- **#1 free app** in Health & Fitness (NL App Store) around launch (July 2025).
- **~4,300 installs/month**, and **~45% register** — well above benchmark. The demand is real.
- **Up to 40% of summaries get shared.** Sharing is the growth engine, not a nice-to-have.
- Backed by **Menzis** (~2M insured) and **Chris Oomen**; **seed round closed Q2 2026**.

## The core challenge (why brand matters now)

- Our north star is **Core MAU**: people who viewed a summary in the last 30 days.
- The **"aha" moment is viewing a summary**. People who reach it come back; people who view two in their first month retain far better.
- But **~88% of registered users never view a summary**. The bottleneck is **activation and retention, not acquisition**.
- Targets in motion: **D14 activation 11% → 25%**, **MAU retention 40% → 60%**.

## Where we're going (Q2 2026 OKRs)

| Month | Core MAU |
|---|---|
| March | 1,381 |
| April | 2,058 |
| May | 3,066 |
| June (target) | 4,568 |

- **Year-end goal: 50,000 Core MAU.**
- **Care circle size: 1.34 → 3.4** (the average Dutch family). The care circle is both a retention mechanism and a second path to activation: a caretaker can hit the aha moment through a parent's or partner's appointment.
- **Focus: critical health moments, oncology first** (also neurodegenerative and sudden severe diagnosis).
- **Expansion:** the Netherlands now; **UK next**, then Spain, Ireland, Germany, Italy.

## Why this is a brand job

Healthcare has never had a great consumer brand. Trust is the whole game, and trust is built or lost at every touchpoint: the app, the website, social, and the summary itself. A brand that is consistent, recognizable, and trusted is a direct lever on activation, sharing, and retention. That is the line connecting your work to every number on this page.
