# The team and how we work

## Who you're joining

Ditto is around 10 people in Rotterdam, with little patience for the status quo and a shared appetite for shipping fast. We've hired ex-Google folks for AI and Storytel's Head of Growth for growth. The bar is high and the pace is real.

You report to **Merlijn** (founder), and you'll work most closely day to day with **Merlijn, Ilayda, and Niek**. On design, you're joining a **UX Lead** and a **part-time UI designer**, and you bring brand and creative leadership alongside them. The default isn't "add headcount", it's work smarter and more AI-native.

## How we work

A few things that are true about us:

- **Build more, talk less.** We'd rather ship a rough thing and learn than polish a deck about it.
- **Warp speed.** Things move fast and nothing is fully stable. That's a feature, not a bug, and it's why we hired someone who thrives in it.
- **AI-native by default.** AI lets us move faster and leaves more room for taste and craft. Use it.
- **Own it completely.** We hired you for distinct expertise. We expect full ownership, initiative, and pushback where it's warranted.

## What we offer (the human bits)

Skyline view of Rotterdam from the office, coffee from a Moccamaster, competitive salary plus equity, real flexibility, and Pokémon merch. You help shape the culture, including the fair-use stuff like holiday.

## A note on the vibe

We're ambitious and we move fast, but the work is serious: we're designing for people on the hardest days of their lives. Energy and gravity at the same time. That tension runs through the brand, and it'll run through how you work here too.
