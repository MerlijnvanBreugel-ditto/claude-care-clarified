## First month focus

### Brand strategy
- A workshop with the founders, Niek, and Ilayda within four weeks on brand identity, focused on content and positioning. → `s5-workshop`
- A proposal for what Ditto as a brand should become, how the position should be shaped, and which values and identity it should reflect. → `s5-brand-direction`
- Interviews with the team and users to understand everyone's perception of the brand today. → `s5-interviews`
- A draft brand identity strong enough to begin experimenting with in month two and to translate into concrete deliverables. → `s5-brand-direction`
- A long-term strategic perspective on how the brand should develop through year-end, connected to oncology, expansion, and product enrichment. → `s5-longterm`

### Brand creative output
- A cadence of at least one weekly creative asset, with room to explore, experiment, and deviate from the current brand. → `s5-creative-cadence`
- A logo animation concept. → `s5-logo-animation`
- Social: with a performance marketeer (and a social media manager, open vacancy), a concrete plan to build the brand on socials, including 2+ experiments positioning Ditto in different ways. → `s5-social-plan`
- Proven and tested workflows of AI tooling for (brand) design, with Ilayda and Inês. Not testing for the sake of it: the bar is real gains in speed, experimentation, or quality. → `s5-ai-tooling`

### Shaping and owning your role
- A clear plan for how she'll shape brand and the brand lead role over the first three months: what she'll deliver, and when. We onboarded her to fully own brand. → `s5-ownership-plan`
- An outline for how ownership of brand work is taken on, how the team is involved, what they should know, and how quality control on brand exposure works. → `s5-ownership-plan`

> Also built in: the **3-week evaluation** check-in (`s5-3week-eval`).

## First 3 months focus

Captured as a forward-look reading (`s5-first-90-days`) rather than checkboxes, so it doesn't read as "overdue" inside the month-one view.

- A brand identity, brandbook, and brand system that establishes a solid core.
- A brand positioning localized for one of our expansion countries.
- A branding campaign in that country.
- A full team onboarding and alignment process, so everyone can dream in, and leverage, the brand system.
- A recognizable, consistent brand on social (primarily Instagram and LinkedIn, maybe more later).
- A roughly 50/50 split between strategizing/shaping the brand and hands-on creative output.

## Expectations (the spirit)

Don't over-plan the next six months in detail. Things will change. Focus on concrete deliverables that create value now, internally and externally. The expectation is full ownership, initiative, and pushback where warranted. The role was hired for distinct skills and expertise, and that should come through clearly.
