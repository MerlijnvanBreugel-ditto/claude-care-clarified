What's Happening in AI

The Agent Era

Open-source projects like ZeroClaw and NanoClaw are proving a new paradigm: AI assistants that don't just respond — they remember, anticipate, and act.

Persistent Memory

The assistant remembers everything. Your meds, your doctors, your worries. Not "start from scratch" every time.

Proactive Tasks

It doesn't wait for you to ask. It reminds, nudges, flags, checks in. The assistant comes to you.

Tool Use

It calls external services: drug databases, calendars, translation. The AI orchestrates, not just generates.

We don't need to build ZeroClaw. We need to bring these principles into Ditto.

The Concept

Ditto becomes a personal health companion

Same app. Same brand. But powered by Claude's intelligence, with agent capabilities that make every interaction smarter than the last.

Patient speaks → Claude understands → Profile builds → Ditto acts

Why Claude Specifically

Not Just "Better AI"
— Different Capabilities

1M Token Context

Hold an entire care journey in memory during one interaction. 18 months of cancer treatment context, all available at once. No summarization loss.

Tool Use & MCP

Claude doesn't just generate text — it calls drug databases, checks calendars, queries translation APIs. Orchestration, not just generation.

Nuanced Communication

Same information, five different audiences. Partner gets clinical detail. Elderly parent gets simplified Dutch. Friend abroad gets English. Claude adapts tone, not just words.

Extended Thinking

For complex medical reasoning: "This new prescription + your existing meds + the conversation last month = something worth flagging." Careful, step-by-step reasoning.

Strong Dutch

Medical terminology, code-switching between Dutch and English medical terms, dialect variation. Needs validation, but the foundation is strong.

Constitutional Safety

Built-in refusal to diagnose, triage, or provide medical advice. In healthcare, the AI that knows what NOT to say is more valuable than the one that says more.

Eyes Wide Open

What Claude Cannot Do

Credibility comes from knowing the limits. These aren't problems to solve — they're boundaries to design around.

Medical Triage

Never determine urgency. Never assess severity. Always: "Contact your GP or 112." Hardcoded, not prompt-engineered.

Drug Safety

Medication interactions must come from verified databases (KNMP G-Standaard), not LLM knowledge. Claude explains the result — it doesn't generate it.

Push Notifications

Claude is an API — it responds, it doesn't initiate. App owns the scheduling layer. Claude decides what/when; app delivers.

EHR Access

Can't log into hospital systems. Until EHDS (2029): recording + documents + voice = the input layer.

Persistent Memory

Every API call starts fresh. The app IS the memory layer — health profile, conversation history, context injection.

100% Dutch Medical NLP

Medication names, abbreviations, code-switching. Strong but not perfect. Validation step mandatory for anything safety-critical.

Risks

Four Things We Must Get Right

GDPR & Data Residency

Health data is GDPR special category (Art. 9). Sending it to Claude's API needs: data processing agreement with Anthropic, EU endpoint confirmation, explicit patient consent. This is the first thing to validate.

Medical Hallucination

Claude must never present general knowledge as fact about YOUR health. Every answer grounded in: "based on your conversation with Dr. X" or "according to your lab results." Data, not inference.

Over-Trust

Users will treat Ditto as a medical authority. Design must reinforce: "I organize your health information. Doctors make decisions." Clear, consistent, unavoidable.

Cost at Scale

1M context window per interaction is expensive. 35K users × multiple daily interactions = significant API cost. Smart context management, caching, and tiered usage are essential.

Next Steps

Five Things to Validate

Before building anything. Cheap tests, expensive answers.

1. GDPR & Data Residency

Does Anthropic offer EU-hosted Claude endpoints? What does the data processing agreement look like for health data?

2. Claude vs. Azure OpenAI on Dutch Medical Text

Run the same 50 conversations through both. Compare: summarization quality, extraction accuracy, medication name accuracy, cost per call.

3. Contextual Q&A Prototype

Take 10 real summaries. Let Claude answer follow-up questions. Is the quality good enough to ship? This is the lowest-infrastructure, highest-wow job.

4. Cost Modeling

Project Claude API costs at current usage patterns. How does 1M context pricing work? What's the per-user monthly cost?

5. User Appetite

Show 5 patients the top 10 jobs list. Which ones make their eyes light up? Which ones do they not care about? Test before you build.
