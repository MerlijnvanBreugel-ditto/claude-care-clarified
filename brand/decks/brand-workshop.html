Why Brand

A brand is not a logo.
It's a decision-making system.

It determines who trusts you, who joins you, who pays you.

"A brand is a person's gut feeling about a product, service, or company."
— Marty Neumeier, The Brand Gap
Why Now

EU expansion ahead.

Inspiration

Lessons from brands
we admire.

Headspace

Brand IS the product. One visual metaphor, applied everywhere. Unmistakable.

Lesson: be so distinctive no one confuses you for anyone else.

Apple

Radical simplicity. Says less than you'd expect. Hits harder because of it.

Lesson: simplicity is a brand position, not just a design preference.

Patagonia

The mission is the brand. They'd rather lose a sale than compromise a value.

Lesson: values without sacrifice are just decoration.

1 — Purpose

Why does Ditto exist?

Select the one that feels most true.

Empowerment. The patient as protagonist.

B — Healthcare should bring people closer, not push them apart.

E — No one should go through healthcare feeling alone or in the dark.

2 — Values

Four convictions.
Proven by decisions.

Values aren't aspirations. They're beliefs we've already paid for.

The patient unlocks everything

We build for the person, not the system.

Proof: said no to white-labeling. Consumer-first, always.

Health is never solo

A diagnosis lands on a family. We build for the circle.

Proof: built sharing before monetization. 39% shared.

Simplicity is the feature

If it makes the product harder to understand, it doesn't ship.

Proof: said no to elderly-only mode. Accessible for all.

Earn trust, don't extract it

We never sell data or use it in ways users wouldn't expect.

Proof: rejected pharma data monetization.

4 — The Contract

Always. Never.

Ditto always:

Makes you walk away understanding, not guessing

Helps the people who care about you actually help

Ensures nothing important gets lost or forgotten

Ditto never:

Sells or exploits your health data

Makes a vulnerable moment more stressful

Sounds like the system that confused you

5 — Voice Rules

Four rules for how
Ditto speaks.

Clear before clever.

If a sentence explains, it stays. If it only entertains, cut it.

Warm before clinical.

A friend who understands, not a system that processes.

Light touch, not light subject.

Moments of delight in heavy contexts — a smile, not a joke.

Never sound like the system.

No jargon, no bureaucratic tone, no talking at people.

Tagline

Care. Clarified.

Short. Strong. Rhythm. Focused.

Captures clarity. Doesn't fully capture connection.
Good enough to build on.
