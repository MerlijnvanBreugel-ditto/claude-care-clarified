# Typography

## Web / Digital (current standard)

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings | Bricolage Grotesque | 500–600 | Google Font, variable optical size |
| Body | Inter | 400 | Google Font, system-level fallback |
| Labels | Inter | 600, uppercase | Letterspacing +2.5px |

## Print / PDF (from brandbook)

| Role | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| All | Uncut Sans | Light, Regular, Semibold |
| Fallback (MS Office) | Aptos | Regular, Bold |

Heading hierarchy uses "perfect fourth" ratio (~1.333) between steps.

Recommendation from brandbook: share as PDF rather than PPT to preserve fonts.
