# Graphic & Visual Elements

## Photography (primary visual language)

Editorial, warm, film-grain quality. This is how Ditto looks externally.

- **Real people** in real settings — not stock-feeling
- **Warmth and dignity** — connection between people, life moments
- **Subjects**: older adults, families, care moments, everyday life
- **Treatments**: full-bleed hero, inset with rounded corners (`20px radius`), split (text + photo)
- **Overlays**: dark navy gradients for text legibility on photos — bottom-up by default, stronger for bright images
- **Tone**: calm confidence, not clinical; intimate, not institutional

## Flexible "O" (brand element)

The logo's 'o' is a dynamic, organic shape — always in motion. Symbolizes adaptability and the evolving nature of care. Can be used as standalone graphic at large scale.

## Illustrations (in-app only)

Minimalist line drawings with teal/mint gradient fills. Characters have no faces for universal identification. Used in product UI — not in external brand or pitch materials.

## Logo

- White version on dark/photo backgrounds
- Dark blue version on light backgrounds
- Minimum size: 12px height
- Preferred placement: top-right, subtle (24px height in decks)
