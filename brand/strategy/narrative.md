# Pitch Narrative

> Extracted from the Mar 2026 pitch deck (latest) and Jan 2026 Balderton deck

## The story arc

### 1. Origin (WHY)
Two quotes, back to back:

**Riet (grandmother):** "I remember less than half of what the doctor said, so I don't know what to do. That's why I always bring my grandchild with me."

**Merlijn (founder, grandson):** "After the visit, the *real work* begins: informing the 28 family members via the phone tree. What did the doctor say? What are we going to do now?"

This is the founder story. It's personal, it's real, and it frames the problem as inherently multi-person.

### 2. Problem
Healthcare is communicated in the **worst possible way**:
- 60% of a consult is forgotten
- <25% of medical info is rightly understood
- 82% of patients are unable to update loved ones
- +38% increase in 5-year mortality rate

...driving poorer outcomes and surging costs.

### 3. Thesis
The biggest breakthrough in healthcare will not come from new drugs, data systems, or devices. It is giving people the **right information**, in the **right format**, at the **right time**.

### 4. Solution
Ditto launches the **communication layer** consumers have been missing:
- **Capture** — record conversations, upload documents and health records, on-device
- **Clarify** — responsible AI turns medical jargon into understandable text, voice, image
- **Connect** — share your journey safely to keep friends and family updated

### 5. Social care
The core visual: doctor at the center, patient and care circle around them. Same medical information, translated differently for each person — plain language for the elderly parent, audio summary for the French-speaking relative, simplified explanation for the child. This is "social care."

### 6. Traction
- #1 Free App in Health & Fitness (NL App Store)
- 71K installs (was 54K in Jan), 11.1% WoW growth
- 25% referral source from family & friends
- 39% of summaries shared, 1.8x higher engagement after sharing
- Menzis partnership (~2M insured)
- National press: Het Parool, AD, NOS Radio 1, Quote, Emerce, Skipr

### 7. Vision
**Ditto: becoming the European healthcare consumer layer.**

The "consumer layer" analogy: Google Maps and Spotify transformed raw infrastructure (maps data, music catalogs) into human-centered experiences and became the interface through which people actually engaged with complex systems. Healthcare has no such layer. Patient portals are unmanageable, records are fragmented, and loved ones are systematically excluded.

**Ambition: Polarsteps of healthcare.**

### 8. Product pillars
UX that's **clear, convenient, and connected**:

| Pillar | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Clearly visualized, structured & insightful overview of your care |
| **Convenience** | Support before, during, and after medical visits |
| **Connection** | Shared understanding and thoughtful platform for connection |

### 9. Product evolution
The patient scribe is the **entry point** to expand into the full care journey:
- **Now** — Single usage (point-in-time during appointments)
- **Next** — Shared care (care circles can support and coordinate)
- **Future** — Persistent value (context accumulates across visits, providers, and time)

*"The one who owns the user, owns the patient, owns the opportunities."*

### 10. Focus
**80% consumer**: starting where care is complex, emotional, and shared — scored on fragmentation, emotional load, social dependency, recording acceptance. Top verticals: onco-hematology (highest fit), neurodegenerative (MS, Parkinson), pregnancy.

**20% B2B**: integrate with modern players (medical scribes, health insurance) for speed and credibility. Legacy EHR has distribution but zero trust, integration, or speed.

### 11. Goals (current phase)
**Proving what compounds — faster:**
1. Prove growth loops (which moments/journeys actually compound)
2. Increase execution speed (fewer bets, bigger bets, faster shipping)
3. Test strategic monetization (only when it supports user growth)

### 12. Expansion
Driven by recording consent and culture. Priority markets: UK (#1), Spain, Ireland, Germany, Italy.

### 13. Funding
Raised €2.2M pre-seed (SAFEs). Raising €5-6M seed for 18-24 months of focused execution. Tranches: €1.4M H1 '26, €1.7M H2 '26, €2.3M H1 '27. Target: global consumer investor + healthtech partner + strategic funds.

## Narrative vs. consumer deck (what we built today)

The pitch deck is investor-facing: data-heavy, traction-forward, with product screenshots. The consumer deck is vision-facing: emotional, photography-driven, fewer stats, more story. Both share the same core arc (problem → thesis → Care Circle → pillars → moat → expansion) but the consumer deck drops the traction slides, team slides, and funding ask in favor of the "why now" timing argument and the competitive framing against Big Tech.
