# Brand Positioning

> Sources: Confluence (Brand positioning, Feb 2026), pm-workspace/context/brand-positioning.md

## Mission
We clarify and connect care journeys for you and your loved ones.

## Tagline
**Care. Clarified.**

## Manifest (Radical Product Thinking)

**Today, when** people face healthcare challenges, **they want to** understand what's happening, know what to do, and loop in their loved ones, **but they have to** navigate a healthcare communication crisis where most of what doctors say is forgotten or misunderstood at the most crucial moments.

**This is unacceptable because** in a world where everything seems connected, people still can't easily access, understand, or share their own medical information. It's easier to follow your niece's gap year than your mother's healthcare journey.

**We envision a world where** people navigate healthcare with clarity and connection — where medical information is always understandable and flows seamlessly within your care circles, transforming anxiety into confidence and confusion into action.

**We're bringing this world about through** Ditto, the Polarsteps of healthcare — turning an overwhelming journey into something you can understand, document, and share with your loved ones.

## Brand Proposition

Ditto transforms confusion into confidence by putting your complete health story in your pocket and connecting your care circle when you need them most:
- **Capture** every aspect of your health in one secure place
- **Clarify** medical information through simple, human explanations
- **Connect** with your care circle, always on your terms

*"This isn't another medical portal. It's healthcare reimagined — where clarity and connection so you can act, rather than be patient."*

## Key Differentiators
1. **Patient-First Design** — built from the person's perspective, not the provider's
2. **Understanding, Not Just Access** — transforms complex info into clear, actionable insights
3. **Cross-System Integration** — bridges gaps between healthcare systems
4. **Customizable Care Networks** — personalized support networks, not system-designated
5. **Privacy by Architecture** — health data under your control, not exposed to us or third parties

## Audience Segments

| Priority | Segment | Core pain | How Ditto helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Older people with increased care needs | Comprehension difficulties, scattered info, digital insecurity | Unified platform, plain language, simplified interface |
| Secondary | Children, family, caregivers | Fragmented data, messenger burden, emotional impact | Simple sharing with permissions, comprehensive health narrative |
| Tertiary | People in intense care journeys (cancer) | Emotional overload, high information complexity | Multi-specialist unification, journey tracking, sharing tools |
| Quaternary | People with language barriers | Comprehension, system unfamiliarity, cultural differences | Plain language, visual explanations, translation |

## Vision Statement
Ditto: becoming the **European healthcare consumer layer.** Like Google Maps transformed raw map data and Spotify transformed music catalogs — Ditto transforms fragmented healthcare into a human-centered experience. The interface through which people actually engage with their care.

**Ambition: Polarsteps of healthcare.**

## Category-Creation Language
Ditto is the **communication layer** consumers have been missing. Not another patient portal. Not a health chatbot. The social layer that connects patients with the people who care about them.

*"The one who owns the user, owns the patient, owns the opportunities."*

## Competitive Framing
The consumer deck positions Ditto against Big Tech health AI (Apple, OpenAI, Microsoft, Google) with a clear distinction: **they build for individuals; care is fundamentally multi-person.** The defensible asset is not the AI — it's the Care Circle.

The pitch deck adds: legacy EHR (Epic etc.) has distribution but zero trust, integration, or speed. Health insurers have trust but can't ship. Medical scribes integrate fast but lack distribution. Ditto sits at the intersection.
