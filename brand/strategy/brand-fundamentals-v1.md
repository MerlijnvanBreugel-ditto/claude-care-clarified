## Purpose

**We exist to make sure no one goes through healthcare in the dark.**

Patients forget, families worry, systems don't communicate. We believe the assumption that patients just can't understand healthcare is wrong — it's a design failure, not a human limitation. True healthcare outcomes happen outside the consultation room: in the kitchen explaining a diagnosis to your partner, in the group chat updating your siblings, in the quiet moment at home re-reading what the doctor said.

Ditto exists to make those moments better.

---

## Values

Four convictions we already act on — even when it costs us.

### 1. The patient unlocks everything
The healthcare system is built for the system. We build for the person inside it. When you empower the patient, the family can help, the doctor gets a better partner, and outcomes improve. The unlock is never a better dashboard for the provider — it's a clearer picture for the person going through it.

*This is why we said no to white-labeling and why we'll always be consumer-first.*

### 2. Health is never solo
A diagnosis doesn't land on one person. It lands on a family, a friend group, a care circle. Every product decision we make must account for the people around the patient — not as an add-on, but as core to how care actually works.

*This is why we built sharing before monetization, and why 39% of summaries are shared.*

### 3. Simplicity is the feature
In healthcare, complexity is the default. We strip it away relentlessly. If a feature makes the product harder to understand, it doesn't ship. If a screen can say it in fewer words, it should. Simplicity isn't a design preference — it's how we deliver clarity to people in stressful moments.

*This is why we said no to designing exclusively for elderly — we chose accessible for everyone over optimized for one group.*

### 4. Earn trust, don't extract it
We handle data from people at their most vulnerable. We will never sell it, monetize it behind their backs, or use it in ways they wouldn't expect. We give users control over what they share and with whom. Privacy isn't a feature — it's the price of admission.

*This is why we rejected pharma data monetization when it was the easy revenue path.*

## Position

**Ditto is the care companion that makes healthcare understandable and shareable.**

We're not a patient portal (those are built for systems, not people). We're not a health chatbot (those are built for individuals, not families). We're not a quantified-self app (those are built for the healthy and curious, not the sick and overwhelmed).

Ditto sits where no one else does: at the intersection of **patient clarity** and **family connection**.

**Category:** Social care platform — a new category that puts the patient and their circle at the center, not the provider or the insurer.

**One word to own:** Understanding.

---

## Promise

**After every healthcare moment, you and your people will understand what happened, what it means, and what to do next.**

What Ditto always delivers:
- You walk away understanding, not guessing
- The people who care about you can actually help
- Nothing important gets lost or forgotten

What Ditto never does:
- Sell or exploit your health data
- Make a vulnerable moment more stressful
- Sound like the system that confused you in the first place

## Personality

### Who Ditto is
Relatively young but knowledgeable. Radiates comfort — not low-energy, but thoughtful and uplifting. Feels immediately familiar, but brings something new. Not gendered. The kind of person you'd want next to you in a waiting room: calm enough to steady you, smart enough to explain things, warm enough that you don't feel like a case number.

### How Ditto speaks

| Dimension | Where we land |
|---|---|
| Formal ←→ Casual | Casual — but never sloppy |
| Optimistic ←→ Realistic | Optimistic — grounded, not naive |
| Loud ←→ Quiet | Quiet — confident doesn't need volume |
| Playful ←→ Serious | Playful — with limits. Delight, not comedy |
| Teacher ←→ Friend | Friend — who happens to know things |
| Premium ←→ Accessible | Accessible — but with taste |

### Voice rules
- **Clear before clever.** If a sentence explains, it stays. If it only entertains, cut it.
- **Warm before clinical.** We're a friend who understands, not a system that processes.
- **Light touch, not light subject.** Moments of delight in heavy contexts — a smile, not a joke.
- **Never sound like the system.** No jargon, no bureaucratic tone, no talking *at* people.

### Ditto never sounds like
- A clinical system explaining your results
- An aggressive startup disrupting your health
- A patronizing app dumbing things down
- An AI pretending to have feelings it doesn't have
