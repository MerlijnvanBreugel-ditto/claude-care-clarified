# Brand Fundamentals Workshop

Answer each question directly. Short is fine. Skip what you don't know — gaps are data too.

---

## 1. PURPOSE — Why does Ditto exist?

Not the mission statement. The deeper reason.

**1.1** If Ditto succeeds wildly — 50M users, every EU country — what's actually different about the world? Not for Ditto. For people.

**1.2** What pisses you off about how healthcare works today? Not the business opportunity — the genuine anger or frustration that makes you keep going when it's hard.

**1.3** Finish this sentence: "We started Ditto because we couldn't accept that ___."

**1.4** What would you want a patient to say about Ditto to their doctor? Not a review — literally what words they'd use in that conversation.

**1.5** Is Ditto ultimately about the patient? The family? The relationship between them? Where does the center of gravity sit?

---

## 2. VALUES — What does Ditto believe to be true?

Values aren't aspirations. They're convictions you already act on — even when it costs you.

**2.1** What have you said NO to? A feature, a partnership, a revenue model, a hire — something that would have been easy to say yes to, but felt wrong. List 2-3.

**2.2** What would a competitor do that would make you think "they don't get it"? What line would they cross that Ditto never would?

**2.3** Name a tension where Ditto deliberately picks a side:
- Simple vs. Comprehensive — do you cut features to stay simple, or add them because users need them?
- Fast vs. Careful — do you ship fast and iterate, or ship right the first time?
- Consumer-first vs. Provider-friendly — when these conflict, who wins?
- Privacy-absolute vs. Privacy-pragmatic — would you sacrifice a useful feature to protect privacy?

**2.4** What do you believe about healthcare that most healthtech founders would disagree with?

**2.5** What do you believe about AI that most AI companies would disagree with?

**2.6** Complete: "Other companies in our space optimize for ___. We optimize for ___."

---

## 3. POSITION — What space does Ditto own?

Position is what people say when you're not in the room.

**3.1** When someone asks "what is Ditto?" at a dinner party, what do you actually say? Not the pitch deck answer — the real one.

**3.2** What is Ditto's #1 competitor in the user's mind? Not another app — the actual current behavior they use instead. (WhatsApp groups? Calling mom? A notebook? Nothing?)

**3.3** You use "consumer layer" and "Polarsteps of healthcare" in investor contexts. Do these resonate with actual users too, or do users describe Ditto differently? How?

**3.4** If Ditto had to pick ONE word to own in people's minds, what would it be? Not a tagline — a single word that, when someone thinks it, they think Ditto.

**3.5** What category is Ditto in? And if the answer is "we're creating a new one" — what are the two existing categories you sit between?

---

## 4. PROMISE — What can people always count on?

The promise is the contract. Break it once, trust is gone.

**4.1** A user opens Ditto for the first time after a scary diagnosis. What must that experience deliver? What's the minimum that earns their trust?

**4.2** What's the one thing Ditto should ALWAYS do, even if it means the product is slower, harder to build, or less profitable?

**4.3** What's the one thing Ditto should NEVER do?

**4.4** If Ditto had a guarantee — like Domino's "30 minutes or free" — what would it be? (Not literally, but the spirit of it.)

**4.5** Think of your happiest user. What did Ditto give them that nothing else could?

---

## 5. PERSONALITY — How does Ditto show up?

Not just tone of voice — the full character.

**5.1** Ditto walks into a room. Who is this person? Age, energy, how do they dress, how do they talk? Don't overthink — first instinct.

**5.2** Pick the side of each spectrum where Ditto leans:
- Formal ←→ Casual
- Optimistic ←→ Realistic
- Loud ←→ Quiet
- Playful ←→ Serious
- Teacher ←→ Friend
- Premium ←→ Accessible

**5.3** What should Ditto NEVER sound like? A brand, a persona, a tone — what makes you cringe?

**5.4** Read these two versions of the same message. Which is more "Ditto"?

> **A:** "Your appointment summary is ready. We've translated the medical terms so you can understand what was discussed and share it with your family."

> **B:** "Here's what your doctor said — in words that actually make sense. Share it with whoever needs to know."

**5.5** What humor, if any, is allowed? Where? (App? Social media? Investor decks? Nowhere?)

---

## 6. BONUS — The hard questions

These aren't about brand definition. They're about brand tension — the places where your instincts might pull in two directions.

**6.1** Ditto's users are often in vulnerable, emotional moments. But your brand energy is confident and fresh. How do you show up with energy without being tone-deaf to someone who just got a cancer diagnosis?

**6.2** You want to be the "Headspace of healthcare" in distinctiveness — but Headspace built its brand on one visual metaphor (meditation illustrations). Ditto's visual identity is still evolving. What's the ONE visual idea that could be Ditto's equivalent of Headspace's illustrations?

**6.3** "Care. Clarified." — is this the permanent tagline, or could something better exist? What do you love about it? What's its limit?

**6.4** The name "Ditto" — what does it mean to you? How do you want people to interpret it? (Same as you, echo, repeat, agreement?)

---

*Take these one section at a time. Write as much or as little as feels right. The gaps and the strong answers are equally useful.*
