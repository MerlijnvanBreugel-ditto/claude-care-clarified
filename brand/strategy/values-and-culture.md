# Brand Values & Culture

> Sources: Confluence (Culture, Brand positioning)

## Brand Values

| # | Value | Internal (Culture) | External (Positioning) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mission Above All** | Every decision advances the mission. High standards are the standard. Exceptional talent, exceptional work. | Transform healthcare by making the complex understandable. Care, Clarified. |
| 2 | **Humans over Systems** | Only a healthy team can create magic. Team wins together. Tough on problems, kind to people. | Built for humans, not systems. Every feature starts with your needs. |
| 3 | **Upward and Onwards** | Build more, talk less. Ship fast, learn faster. Work smarter with AI and automation. | Embrace boldness for meaningful change. Challenge conventions. |
| 4 | **For Everyone** | Seek out challenging viewpoints. Diverse users, diverse team. Inclusion is how we operate. | No barriers. Your health, your network, your way. |
| 5 | **Empowered Action** | Own it completely. Take responsibility. Execution beats everything. | Clarity becomes the foundation for empowerment. |

## Culture Principles

1. **Mission above all** — if it doesn't advance the mission, we don't do it
2. **Culture is built daily** — every hire, decision, and interaction either strengthens or weakens it
3. **Build more, talk less** — default to action, use AI, ship fast, iterate
4. **Team wins together** — tough on problems, kind to people, ego at the door
5. **Diversity drives innovation** — different perspectives create better solutions
6. **Own it completely** — bring solutions, own mistakes, move forward

## Brand Personality

- **Clear & Direct** — eliminate jargon, reject fluff, speak plainly
- **Empathetic & Personal** — recognize emotional weight, lean personal and committed
- **Bold & Authentic** — challenge conventions when they don't serve patients
- **Trustworthy & Credible** — privacy by conviction, not regulation; don't monetize data
