# Ditto Brand Onboarding

**How we see the Ditto brand today: where we are, where we came from, and what is still open.**

This is the single starting point for the brand effort (the incoming brand lead first, the wider team second). It pulls together everything that defines how Ditto looks, sounds, and feels, points to the source files, and is honest about what is actually settled versus what is still a working draft versus what is still a sketch.

> **Read this as a map of a brand mid-construction.** Very little is locked. The few things that are settled are the things we already say in public (the mission, the way we pitch Ditto in the open Brand Lead role, the founder story). Almost everything about expression, personality, and the visual system is still being worked out, on purpose. That is the job to be done.

## How we describe Ditto today (public)

🟢 This is the live framing from the open **Brand Lead role** (`brand-lead-role.md`). It is what we put in front of candidates and the market, so it is the closest thing we have to an agreed external story.

> **Ditto-what?** "Let's bring convenience to care. Ditto is building the consumer layer that European healthcare has been missing, by providing people the right info, in the right format, at the right time."
>
> **Why?** Your grandmother brings a child to every hospital visit because she can't fully grasp or recall what was said. Or you stitch your history together across ten apps and portals, in Latin. Healthcare is designed for providers and leaves people passive and ill-understood. It forces us to be *patient*. We put you and your loved ones at the heart of care.
>
> **What?** An AI-powered app that clarifies every healthcare encounter and connects you with the people around you through the Care Circle. The start of a platform to navigate healthcare with convenience and clarity. "The Polarsteps for Health, and yes, at least European."

**The brand ambition** (🟢, also from the role doc, and a strong north star for the brand lead):

> Healthcare brands pick a side: clinical and trusted, or warm and shallow. Ditto refuses the choice. The brand should feel "human without being soft, innovative without losing credibility, personal enough to sit with someone during a difficult diagnosis, and universal enough to work across cultures and generations." It must "earn trust in living rooms, waiting rooms, and boardrooms alike." Evolution over revolution: timeless, never rigid.

*Note: an earlier "brand in one breath" summary I'd drafted has been removed. It described Ditto in a voice and as "photography-forward" in ways we don't actually use. The framing above is the real one.*

# 3. Brand fundamentals

The strategy layers in detail, each with an honest status. This is the part to sort into *aligned / draft / open*. Tags are pre-filled; correcting them is the main review job.

## 3.1 Purpose — why Ditto exists

> **We exist to make sure no one goes through healthcare in the dark.**
>
> Patients forget, families worry, systems don't communicate. The assumption that patients just can't understand healthcare is wrong: it's a design failure, not a human limitation. The real outcomes happen outside the consultation room, in the kitchen explaining a diagnosis to a partner, in the group chat updating siblings, in the quiet moment re-reading what the doctor said.

🟢 **Aligned in spirit, can be polished.** The "why" is felt and consistent (it matches the role doc's "Why?"). The exact phrasing is still worth tightening. Source: `brand-fundamentals-v1.md`.

## 3.2 Mission, tagline & vision

| | Statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Mission** | "We clarify and connect care journeys for you and your loved ones." | 🟢 Aligned (shown publicly) |
| **Tagline** | "Care. Clarified." plus a working payoff line we now often use, **"Clarity when it matters most."** | 🟢 in use / 🔴 permanence open |
| **Vision** | "Building the consumer layer European healthcare has been missing… the Polarsteps for Health, at least European." | 🟢 Aligned for the public pitch / 🔴 untested with everyday users |

🔴 The vision language ("consumer layer", "Polarsteps") is in our public pitch, so it's aligned there. Whether everyday users describe Ditto that way is untested.

## 3.3 Positioning

🔴 **Quite implicit, and the biggest open area.** The public version is the role-doc framing above (the consumer layer; right info, right format, right time; for you and your loved ones). That's a *narrative*, not yet a crisp user-facing positioning statement.

We also have a second, more user-centric draft worth keeping in the mix:

> "Ditto is the **care companion** that makes healthcare understandable and shareable, at the intersection of patient clarity and family connection." (🟡 draft, `brand-fundamentals-v1.md`)

**Decision needed:** write *one* explicit positioning a normal user would recognize, distinct from the investor narrative.

**Differentiators** (broadly consistent): patient-first design · understanding, not just access · cross-system integration · the Care Circle · privacy by architecture.

**Competitive framing** (consistent in the pitch): against Big Tech health AI, they build for individuals while care is multi-person (the moat is the Care Circle, not the AI). Against legacy EHR/insurers/scribes, Ditto sits where distribution, trust, and speed intersect.

**One word to own:** "Understanding." 🔴 Up for debate.

### Audience & focus — decided

🟢 **We've decided to focus on critical health moments.** This supersedes the older "older people" framing in `positioning.md`.

- **Primary, short term:** oncology.
- **Also in scope:** neurodegenerative (e.g. MS) and sudden severe diagnosis (e.g. cardiology).
- **The through-line:** moments that are complex, emotional, and shared, where clarity and the care circle matter most.

> ⚠️ `strategy/positioning.md` still leads with "older people with increased care needs." That document needs updating to this decision.

## 3.4 Values — the four convictions

🟡 **Draft, and the wording is up for debate**, but this is the set to use now. Each names something we actually *said no to*, which is what makes them sharper than a generic value list. Source: `brand-fundamentals-v1.md`.

1. **The patient unlocks everything.** *(We said no to white-labeling; we'll always be consumer-first.)*
2. **Health is never solo.** *(We built sharing before monetization; up to 40% of summaries get shared.)*
3. **Simplicity is the feature.** *(We chose accessible-for-everyone over optimized-for-elderly.)*
4. **Earn trust, don't extract it.** *(We rejected pharma data monetization.)*

> The separate **5-value set** in `values-and-culture.md` (Mission Above All · Humans over Systems · Upward and Onwards · For Everyone · Empowered Action) is the internal/culture list. **Treat it as "for later"** and don't merge it into the brand values yet.

## 3.5 Brand promise

> **After every healthcare moment, you and your people will understand what happened, what it means, and what to do next.**
> **Always:** you walk away understanding; the people who care can actually help; nothing important gets lost.
> **Never:** sell or exploit your data; make a vulnerable moment more stressful; sound like the system that confused you.

🟡 **Draft** (`brand-fundamentals-v1.md`). On-message, not yet ratified or tested.

## 3.6 Brand personality

🔴 **Undefined.** Today the brand leans **positive, uplifting, and present**, and that's about as far as agreement goes.

There are two unratified candidate descriptions on file (a trait list in `values-and-culture.md`; a "person at a dinner party" sketch plus spectrums in `brand-fundamentals-v1.md`). They're raw material, not a definition. The real task: move from generic warmth to something only Ditto would say, and possibly toward a calmer register than "uplifting and present." Defining personality is one of the brand lead's first jobs.

## 3.7 Tagline note

"Care. Clarified." is in active use as the brand mark. Alongside it we now often use **"Clarity when it matters most"** (and "Clarity in the moments that matter") as a payoff/headline line. 🔴 Whether "Care. Clarified." is the *permanent* tagline is open. Structurally it stays English in all markets.

# 4. Narrative & story

The working narrative. 🟢 The **origin (the grandmother story)** and the **problem framing** are public (role doc + pitch). 🟡 The rest of the arc is the pitch structure, broadly consistent but not a fixed brand asset. Source: `strategy/narrative.md`.

1. **Origin.** Riet (grandmother): "I remember less than half of what the doctor said." Merlijn (grandson, founder): "After the visit, the real work begins, informing the family." Personal, and multi-person by nature.
2. **Problem.** Healthcare is communicated in the worst possible way: most of a consult is forgotten, little is understood, loved ones are left out.
3. **Thesis.** The breakthrough isn't a new drug or device. It's the right information, in the right format, at the right time.
4. **Solution.** Capture → Clarify → Connect.
5. **Social care.** The same information translated differently for each person in the circle.
6. **Traction (public).** Launched July 2025, hit #1 in the App Store, backed by Menzis and Chris Oomen, ~80,000 users, up to 40% of summaries shared.
7. **Vision.** The European healthcare consumer layer.
8. **Pillars.** Clarity · Convenience · Connection.

**Key phrases:** "Care. Clarified." · "Clarity when it matters most" · "right info, right format, right time" · "the consumer layer European healthcare has been missing" · "the Polarsteps for Health" · "we shouldn't have to be *patient*."

# 5. Verbal identity: voice & tone

🟡 **The most developed part of the brand, but still a documented draft, not a ratified standard.** Sources: `voice-and-tone/brand-voice.md`, `voice-and-tone/localization.md`.

**The idea:** Ditto makes complex things feel obvious by thinking them through, not by dumbing them down. Warm when it matters, precise when it counts. Today the voice in practice leans positive, uplifting, and present.

**Four principles:** clarity with intelligence (clear ≠ simple) · human & warm (talk like a thoughtful friend) · confident, not corporate (don't hedge) · trustworthy by default (specificity is credibility).

**Tone slider:** same voice, register shifts from homepage hero → feature copy → trust/privacy → professionals → error states → social → brand storytelling.

**Words we use vs. avoid:** Understand (not Leverage) · Summary (not Output) · Your doctor (not Healthcare provider) · Care Circle (not Care network) · Recording (not Audio capture) · Private (not Secure).

**Localization (EN + NL):** *adapt, don't translate.* Dutch is the primary market voice (informal "je/jij", faster to the point, ~20% longer compound words). The tagline stays English. Full guide in `voice-and-tone/localization.md`.

⚪ Open: does the in-app copy actually follow this? (Tied to the unaudited experience.)

# 6. Visual identity

🔴 **Exploring.** The direction is calmer and more premium in intent, but the system has grown organically and isn't where we want it. Sources: `visual-design/`.

**What's relatively settled:**
- **Logo:** white on dark, dark-blue on light. The flexible "o" is a dynamic element (and the leading candidate for a single ownable visual idea).
- **Color (current working palette):** Navy `#0D164F` · Teal `#58B6D2` · Gold `#D4A832` (warmth) · Light Blue `#7DD4E8` · Red `#D94F4F` (pain) · Soft BG `#F7F6F3`.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (headings) + Inter (body) on web; Uncut Sans in print.

**Where it's open (don't over-build these yet, just know they're unresolved):**
- We are **not** photography-first today. **Web is moving into photography**; **video on social is getting good traction.**
- **Open question: do we really use the illustration style in the app?** Its role is undecided.
- New colors, photography guidelines, illustration boundaries, and the official type stack are all unformalized.
