The Problem

Section label: Sound familiar?

Card 1 — You forget

You walk out of the doctor's office and your partner asks: "What did they say?" You try to explain, but the details are already slipping. Research shows patients forget 40-80% of what doctors tell them. The more serious the news, the less you remember.

Card 2 — Your family is in the dark

Your daughter calls from abroad. Your mother just had an appointment. "How did it go?" "Fine." But fine doesn't mean fine — it means no one knows how to explain what the doctor actually said.

Card 3 — Someone becomes the project manager

WhatsApp groups. Phone calls after every appointment. Shared Notes. One family member who keeps track of everything — medications, appointments, what each doctor said. That person is exhausted. And they shouldn't have to do it alone.

How Ditto Works (for professionals)

What it means for your practice

Step 1 — Your patient records the consultation

The patient opens Ditto and taps record. Nothing changes for you. No devices to set up, no integrations to configure, no workflow disruption. The patient focuses on the conversation.

Step 2 — AI generates a plain-language summary

After the visit, Ditto produces a structured summary: what was discussed, what the diagnosis means, what the treatment plan is, what happens next. In language the patient understands.

Step 3 — The patient's Care Circle stays informed

Patients share their summary with family members — reducing the "what did the doctor say?" phone calls. Each person in the Care Circle receives information tailored to their role.

Step 4 — Your next appointment starts better

The patient comes back having reviewed their summary, prepared questions, and aligned with their family. Less time repeating, more time progressing.

How Recording Works

What happens when you record

1. You tap record. Ditto captures the audio of your consultation on your phone.

2. The audio is sent for processing. Temporarily — to secure servers in the European Union. This is when the AI creates your summary.

3. Your summary arrives on your device. Plain language, structured, actionable.

4. The audio is deleted from our servers. Immediately after processing. We don't keep it. We can't replay it. It's gone.

5. Your summary stays on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Data Storage

Where your data lives

On your device. Summaries, documents, and your Care Circle connections are stored locally on your phone. Not in our cloud. Not on a server we manage.

EU servers for processing only. When you record a consultation or scan a document, the data is temporarily sent to secure servers in the European Union for AI processing. After processing, it's deleted.

Encrypted in transit and at rest. All data is encrypted when it moves between your device and our servers, and while it's stored on your device.

AI Transparency

What our AI does — and what it doesn't

What it does:

• Transcribes your medical conversation into text
• Generates a plain-language summary: what was discussed, what it means, what's next
• Translates medical jargon into everyday language
• Explains medical documents you scan with your camera

What it does NOT do:

• Provide medical advice or diagnoses
• Recommend treatments
• Replace your doctor's judgment
• Make decisions about your care
• Access your data after processing is complete
Ditto's AI is a translator, not a doctor. It makes information understandable. It doesn't tell you what to do with it.

Clinical Validation

Built with, not just for, healthcare

Ditto collaborates with healthcare professionals and academic institutions to validate the accuracy and usefulness of our AI summaries.

GDPR Compliance

Your rights under EU law

Right to access. You can request a copy of any data we hold about you — though almost everything is already on your device.

Right to deletion. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Right to portability. Your data belongs to you. You can export it whenever you want.

Data Processing Agreement. Healthcare professionals and organizations using Ditto can request a DPA for formal compliance documentation.

What Ditto Does NOT Do

Let's be explicit

We do NOT sell your data. Not to insurers. Not to advertisers. Not to anyone. Ever.

We do NOT share data with your insurer. Even if your insurer partners with us, they cannot see your personal health information.

We do NOT store your audio. It's processed and deleted. We can't replay your conversations.

We do NOT access your summaries. They live on your device. We have no technical ability to read them.

We do NOT provide medical advice. Ditto translates and clarifies. Your doctor provides the care.

FAQ — For Patients

What is Ditto?

Ditto is a free app that helps you understand and manage your healthcare. Record your doctor's visit, get a clear summary in plain language, understand medical letters, and share your health information with the people who matter to you.

How do I record a consultation?

Open Ditto, tap the record button before your appointment starts, and tap stop when it's finished. Within minutes, you'll have a plain-language summary of everything that was discussed.

Do I need my doctor's permission to record?

In the Netherlands, patients have the legal right to record their own medical consultations. We recommend letting your doctor know — most are supportive, and many already recommend Ditto to their patients.

How do I share a summary with my family?

Open any summary and tap "Share." You can invite people to your Care Circle — your partner, children, parents, or anyone you choose. Each person sees what you decide to share with them.

Can I share different information with different people?

Yes. Your Care Circle lets you control who sees what. Your partner might get the full summary. Your child might get only the key updates. You're always in control.

What if I don't want to share anything?

That's fine. Ditto works perfectly as a personal tool. Sharing is optional — it's there when you want it.

How much does it cost?

Ditto is free. No subscriptions, no trial periods, no hidden costs.

Is Ditto available in my language?

Ditto currently works best in Dutch and English. The app can process conversations in other languages — the summary will be generated in your preferred language.

FAQ — For Healthcare Professionals

How does Ditto work in my practice?

Patients use Ditto on their own phone. There's nothing to install, integrate, or configure on your end. Your workflow doesn't change.

Is the AI summary accurate?

Ditto's AI generates summaries that capture the key points of a consultation in plain language. We collaborate with medical professionals and academic institutions (including UMCG) to continuously validate and improve accuracy.

What about patient consent?

Patients control their own recordings. In the Netherlands, patients have the legal right to record consultations. Ditto encourages patients to inform their doctor.

Do I need to do anything differently?

No. Speak naturally. The AI handles medical terminology, multiple speakers, and varying audio quality.

Can I recommend Ditto to my patients?

Yes. Many healthcare professionals already do. If you'd like materials or a demo, talk to our medical team.

I'd like to try it myself. How?

Book a conversation with one of our medical team members — they're fellow doctors who can walk you through it.

FAQ — Privacy & Data

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Summaries, documents, and Care Circle connections live on your phone, not on our servers.

What happens to my audio recording?

The audio is temporarily sent to secure EU servers for processing. After your summary is generated, the audio is deleted. We can't replay it.

Can Ditto read my summaries?

No. We don't have technical access to your summaries. They're stored on your device, encrypted, and under your control.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete any summary, recording, or your entire account at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Does my insurer see my data?

No. Even if your insurer partners with Ditto, they cannot access your personal health information.
