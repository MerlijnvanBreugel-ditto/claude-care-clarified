# Website Messaging Review — Branding Expert

**Date**: 2026-03-27
**Source**: Voice note from branding expert (external)
**Scope**: dittocare.com — overall messaging and copy direction

---

## Summary

The visual brand is strong — evokes ease, clarity, simplicity. The messaging, however, leans too corporate/product-speak and needs to evolve toward something more emotionally immediate and personal for B2C.

## Key Feedback

### What's working

| Element | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Visual brand | Evokes the right emotions: ease, clarity, simplicity |
| "Care. Clarified." as tagline | Works well for B2B/corporate positioning |
| "Because care is something you carry together" | Emotionally resonant, personal, on-brand |
| "Healthcare shouldn't be a puzzle" | Strong B2C framing — speaks to a real feeling |
| "Overwhelming" as a concept | Good emotional territory to claim |

### What needs work

| Current copy | Problem | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|
| "Care. Clarified." (as hero message) | Reads corporate, not personal enough for B2C | "Clarity in the moments that matter" / "Clarity when it matters most" — more emotional, more immediate |
| "Ditto brings all your healthcare moments together in one place" | Launch/product messaging. People don't think in "healthcare moments". "All in one place" is generic. | Lead with emotional framing, not feature description |
| "A simpler way to manage your health" | App-speak. People don't frame their health as something to "manage" like a task. | Reframe around the feeling (overwhelm, confusion) rather than the action (managing) |

## Core Insight

The messaging is **impersonal** — it describes the product rather than speaking to what people feel. The shift needed:

- **From**: Product descriptions and feature-level language
- **To**: Emotional, immediate language that people feel instantly

This isn't about abandoning clarity — it's about making clarity feel personal.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Audit all website copy for "app-speak" and corporate-sounding lines
2. Rewrite hero messaging with emotional framing (test a few variants)
3. Keep "Care. Clarified." as brand tagline but pair it with warmer hero copy
4. Consider A/B testing emotionally-framed vs. current copy on key pages
5. Ask this reviewer if he'd do a deeper dive on specific pages or the full copy deck
