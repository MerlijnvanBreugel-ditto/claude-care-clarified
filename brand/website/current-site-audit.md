## Problems with Current Site

### Structural Issues
1. **38 pages, no clear hierarchy** — Campaign pages, duplicates, and utility pages clutter the sitemap
2. **Duplicate pages** — `/copy-of-privacy-policy`, `/copy-of-how-we-use-ai` suggest Wix copy-paste habits
3. **Mixed languages** — Some pages Dutch, some English, no clear locale strategy
4. **Outdated content** — Blog last updated Aug 2025 (7+ months stale), Android waitlist page likely obsolete
5. **Blank page** exists in sitemap
6. **Vacancy pages** on marketing site (should be separate or on a careers platform)

### Strategic Issues
1. **Feature-led, not story-led** — Homepage describes what Ditto does, not why it matters or how it feels
2. **No Care Circle narrative** — The biggest differentiator is buried in feature pages, not central to the story
3. **B2B is an afterthought** — `/healthcare-partners` exists but likely has low traffic and weak narrative
4. **No social proof depth** — Press logos but no user stories, outcome data, or partner testimonials on homepage
5. **"Free app" positioning** — Leads with free, not with value. Undermines premium brand aspiration
6. **Mission statement is generic** — "Everyone deserves clarity and control" could be any health app
7. **Photography** — Wix site likely doesn't match the editorial photography direction in brand-evolution.md
8. **No conversion optimization** — Download CTA exists but no funnel thinking (who, why now, what next)

### Brand Alignment Issues
1. **Tagline "Care. Clarified." is right** — but the supporting copy doesn't live up to the brand voice standards
2. **Consumer deck narrative is far ahead** — The deck tells the "consumer layer in healthcare" story; the website still tells a "medical recording app" story
3. **The emotional dimension is missing** — No family connection moments, no "when healthcare gets serious" framing
4. **Technology-forward** — "AI summaries" leads the pitch. Brand guidelines say: humanity over technology

---

## What to Carry Forward

- "Care. Clarified." tagline
- The three pillars (record/understand/share) as feature anchors — but elevated with emotional framing
- Press coverage logos as social proof
- Privacy-first messaging
- Free availability (but not as the headline)

## What to Kill

- All duplicate/copy-of pages
- Blank page
- Outdated campaign pages (evaluate each)
- Android waitlist (if Android is live)
- Feature-led homepage structure
- Generic mission statement
- Mixed-language page structure (commit to a locale strategy)

## What's Missing

- Brand story / founding narrative
- Care Circle as a central narrative (not just a feature)
- User stories / testimonials with real emotion
- Outcome data (75K users, 4.7★ rating, domains served)
- Partner credibility section
- Clear B2B pathway
- Editorial photography
- The "consumer layer in healthcare" vision
