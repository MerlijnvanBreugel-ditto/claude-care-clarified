Our Story

A small team in Rotterdam building the consumer layer in healthcare.

It's easier to follow your niece's gap year than your mother's healthcare journey.

That's the sentence that started everything.

We built Ditto because we saw what happens when healthcare gets serious. People leave the doctor's office confused. They call their partner and can't explain what was said. One family member becomes the unofficial project manager — coordinating between specialists, keeping notes, managing everyone's anxiety. And the tools they use? WhatsApp groups. Shared Google Docs. Phone calls after every appointment.

There's a Strava for fitness. A Flo for women's health. A Revolut for banking. There's nothing for the thing that matters most: understanding your own healthcare and bringing the people around you into the picture.

That's what Ditto is. We turn medical conversations into clarity. We connect patients with the people who care about them. We believe healthcare information belongs to the person it's about — and to the people they choose to share it with.

We're just getting started.

— Merlijn van Breugel, Co-founder
What we believe

The patient is the hero, not the technology.

We build for people navigating healthcare, not for the system serving them. Our AI exists to clarify — not to diagnose, advise, or replace doctors.

Understanding is a right, not a luxury.

If you're living through it, you deserve to understand it. Healthcare information should be clear, complete, and in your language.

Care is multi-person.

Healthcare doesn't happen in isolation. Your partner, your children, your parents — they're part of your journey. The tools should reflect that.

Privacy is architecture, not policy.

We don't promise to protect your data — we build systems that make it impossible for us to access it in the first place.
