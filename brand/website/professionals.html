What it means for your practice

Nothing changes for you. Everything changes for your patients.

1

Your patient records the consultation

The patient opens Ditto and taps record. Nothing changes for you. No devices to set up, no integrations to configure, no workflow disruption.

2

AI generates a plain-language summary

After the visit, Ditto produces a structured summary: what was discussed, what the diagnosis means, what the treatment plan is, what happens next. In language the patient understands.

3

The patient's Care Circle stays informed

Patients share their summary with family — reducing the "what did the doctor say?" phone calls. Each person receives information tailored to their role.

4

Your next appointment starts better

The patient comes back having reviewed their summary, prepared questions, and aligned with their family. Less time repeating, more time progressing.
