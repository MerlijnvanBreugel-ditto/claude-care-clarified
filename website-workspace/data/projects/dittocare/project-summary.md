### Value Proposition

### Origin

## Key Features

1. **Medical Appointment Recording**
   - Tap record during doctor visits
   - Transforms conversations into clear, structured notes
   - Never miss crucial health details

2. **Medical Letter Translation**
   - Snap a photo of any medical document
   - Instantly translates complex terminology
   - Plain language you can understand and act on

3. **Secure Health Sharing**
   - Keep support network informed
   - Share via WhatsApp, email, or in-app
   - User controls what and with whom to share

4. **Centralized Health Data**
   - All medical records in one place
   - Always accessible
   - Data stays on device (privacy-first)
