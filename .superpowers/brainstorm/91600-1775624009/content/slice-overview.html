Quality Gate

Gate 1 — End of Week 4

Can a patient record, get a summary with suggestions, tap a suggestion, and get a grounded answer?

  • Summary quality ≥ 95% on 10 sample conversations
  • Suggestions are specific, not generic (manual review)
  • Answers are grounded with source attribution
  • End-to-end latency: summary <10s, answer first token <3s
  • Health profile populated from extraction
  • Event bus traces full pipeline with correlation IDs

Gate 2 — End of Week 8

Does the brain accumulate knowledge? Can a patient update their profile by voice and photo?

  • Profile accuracy ≥ 90% after 3+ recordings (medication, conditions)
  • Voice input correctly extracts and confirms updates
  • Document processing explains letters and lab results
  • Profile versioning and audit log working
  • Conflict detection and user resolution flow works
  • Cross-appointment context in suggestions and answers

Gate 3 — End of Week 11

Does the brain proactively help and adapt for different audiences?

  • Appointment prep generates relevant, grounded prep docs
  • Sharing adaptation produces meaningfully different versions per role
  • Translated content preserves medical accuracy
  • Elderly-friendly: passes usability heuristics audit
  • Full pipeline tracing, no orphan events
  • All prompts pass regression eval suite
