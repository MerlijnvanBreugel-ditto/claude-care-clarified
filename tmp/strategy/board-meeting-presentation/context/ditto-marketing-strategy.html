From linear growth to a compounding engine. Narrow to severe care paths. Reach caretakers through patient magazines and authentic social content. Let the care circle viral loop do the math. 50K MAU is the Series A milestone — 12-18 month horizon (mid-2027), not a year-end cliff.

TL;DR
Go narrow and deep. Target caretakers and patients in severe care paths (oncology, cardiology, neurology, chronic conditions). Two new content pillars: (1) advertorials in patient association magazines, (2) social / UGC content featuring real patients and caretakers. The strategic decision: narrow and deep

Code Red outcome
The founders chose Option A with care circle amplification: narrow product and marketing to high-intensity care paths (oncology, cardiology, neurology) and their care circles. Focus on caretakers as the primary marketing persona. Build toward a compounding growth engine through social/UGC content + care circle virality.

The invite bottleneck
75% of invite links never convert (24.8% acceptance rate, confirmed via Mixpanel — avg time to accept: 12.1 hours). Once someone installs, acceptance is ~100%. This is a friction problem, not a trust problem. Deep links, skip-onboarding for CC members, and landing directly on the shared summary are prerequisites for the engine.
Data quality flag: caretaker tracking broken
user_type_others_care is not being set on new user profiles. 0 of 12,486 recent registrations show as caretakers. Caretaker share of summary_viewed dropped from 7.3% (Dec) to 0.4% (May) — this is a property-setting regression, not a real decline. Fix needed before we can measure the caretaker activation path. Engineering ticket required.

Activation funnel — where users leak

Live data from Mixpanel (last 90 days, 25,753 installs). The funnel shows why narrow targeting matters: we lose 62.5% at install → register, and another 82% of registered users never reach a key action.

Install → Register: 37.5%
Biggest leak

62.5% of installs never register. Trending up (was 42.7% in Dec, now 55.3% in May) but still the widest part of the funnel. Broad campaigns make this worse — April's record installs (10,592) only converted 52.9% vs. May's 55.3% on targeted channels.

Implication for marketing: high-intent channels (magazines, ASA) will have higher register rates. Stop paying for installs that never register.

Appointment → Summary: 33.2%
Key gap

Only 1 in 3 users who create an appointment generate a summary within 30 days. Average time to convert: 3.5 days. This is the 55% gap — the product's core value is one step away but 67% never reach it.

Implication: Customer.io summary reminder flow is the highest single-deliverable impact for activation. Must ship in Q3.

Key action mix — recording dominates

Recording saved is the dominant path (1,570 unique users in May). Manual appointments saw 3.9x growth since Dec. Document scanning is flat — not a growth lever at current product state.

Content strategy: stories that convert

The shift from broad to narrow means our content must speak directly to people navigating severe care paths. Two content pillars, both designed to reach caretakers (the person most likely to set up Ditto).

Pillar 1: Advertorials in patient magazines
Print

The thesis

Patient association magazines are read by people in the middle of their care journey — exactly when Ditto's value is highest. These are the highest-intent placements available in NL. Caretakers read these to understand what their loved one is going through.

Q3: Test phase (Jul-Sep)

4 Tier 1 placements: ReumaMagazine (2M+ audience), MantelKRACHT (national caretaker magazine), Hartbrug (cardiology 6x/yr), Mantelzorgelijk.nl (mediakit ready). Measure install-to-activation quality vs. newspaper baseline.

Q4: Scale phase (Oct-Dec)

Add oncology (NFK network = 22 organizations, 900K+ people), neurology (Parkinson, MS, Hersenletsel). Pursue Patiëntenfederatie NL partnership for cross-condition reach (200+ patient orgs).

Pillar 2: Social / UGC content
Social

The thesis

External feedback was clear: "you are not a consumer brand" — Ditto only has LinkedIn. Social worked in the past for volume but activation was worse. Now with a better product and care circle features, social can become a compounding engine if the content is authentic and targeted.

Q3: Experiment phase

Launch Instagram + Facebook. Content types: (1) Caretaker stories — "How I manage my mother's cancer care" (2) Patient interviews — "Living with Parkinson's: what I wish I knew" (3) Short-form video: recording an appointment, showing the summary, sharing with care circle. Test 3-5 stories, measure engagement + install quality.

Q4: Scale what works

Build UGC pipeline from community. Partner with patient influencers in cardiology/oncology. Cross-promote magazine stories on social. If activation rate holds above 15%, gradually increase paid social spend.

Scenario planning: the path to Series A

50K MAU is the Series A milestone on a 12-18 month horizon (target: mid-2027). That means ~30% MoM sustained — aggressive but within the range of top-performing startups (vs. 57% MoM if it were 7 months). Q3/Q4 2026 builds the engine. H1 2027 is when it compounds.

The math changes with a longer runway
At 30% MoM from 2,083 today: ~5,600 by Dec 2026, ~25,000 by Apr 2027, ~50,000 by Jun 2027. At 20% MoM: ~50K by Oct 2027. The engine doesn't need to be at full speed by December — it needs to be demonstrably compounding so the Series A story is credible.

12-month projections through Jun 2027. Status quo plateaus ~9K. Narrow strategy reaches ~42K by mid-2027. Engine on hits 50K by spring 2027. Q3/Q4 2026 is the inflection period where the engine either starts compounding or doesn't.
