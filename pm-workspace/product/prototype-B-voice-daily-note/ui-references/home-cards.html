Home "Today's Action" Cards

12 references for an inviting home card that pulls you into today's single action — jot a thought, do a check-in, meditate. For ditto: the "ditto today → tap to talk" entry point.

Pattern read: the most inviting ones share four things — a warm time-of-day greeting, one hero card (not a list of options), a plain-language reason ("to refocus and find energy"), and a single soft CTA ("Begin" / "Start" / "How do you feel?"). For ditto, the orb can be the CTA. Three picks to anchor on are flagged green: stoic. (boldest single-action), Bloom (calm hero + personalised), Insight Timer (closest to a daily health check-in).
