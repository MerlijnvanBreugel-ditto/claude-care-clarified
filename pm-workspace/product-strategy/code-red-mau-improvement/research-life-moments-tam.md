# Life Moments TAM: Netherlands & Europe
*Research synthesis · May 2026 · Strategic planning use only*

The addressable market for Ditto is not "everyone with a health app" — it is people actively in specialist care with regular appointments. This document sizes that population for Ditto's four core life moment categories.

---

## Cancer

### Netherlands
| Metric | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New diagnoses/year | ~128,000 (2023, rising) | IKNL / Netherlands Cancer Registry |
| Projected new cases/year by 2033 | ~156,000 | IKNL projection |
| 5-year prevalence (living with/after cancer) | ~600,000–700,000 | ECIS / OECD EU Country Profile 2025 |
| In active treatment or close follow-up (years 1–2) | ~250,000 | Estimated (50% of 5yr prevalence in active phase) |
| NL cancer rate | 602/100,000 — 3rd highest in EU | ECIS 2024 |

Top 5 types by volume: breast, colorectal, prostate, lung, skin (melanoma). All involve specialist appointments: oncology, surgery, radiotherapy, follow-up imaging.

### Europe (EU27)
| Metric | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New diagnoses/year | ~2.7 million (2024) | ECIS / JRC |
| 5-year prevalence | ~20 million | ECIS estimate |
| In active treatment/close follow-up | ~8–10 million | Estimated |

**Ditto relevance:** Cancer patients are Ditto's highest-value segment. They have frequent specialist appointments (weekly–monthly in treatment, quarterly in follow-up), high emotional stakes, and strong motivation to involve family. Appointment recording is deeply natural in oncology — patients often can't process everything they hear in real time.

**Note on Alzheimer's / Dementia:** The patient who records the appointment is often the caregiver (adult child, partner), not the patient themselves. This changes the Ditto use case — it is the caregiver attending and recording, then sharing with other family members. This is actually a stronger care circle activation pattern than other segments.

## Total Addressable Population Summary

### Netherlands — "Active specialist follow-up" estimate

These are people actively seeing a specialist with regular appointments (roughly 2+ per year) — the core Ditto use case.

| Category | Estimated addressable |
|---|---|
| Cancer (in active treatment/close follow-up) | ~250,000 |
| Neurodegenerative (active specialist care) | ~160,000 |
| Cardiac (active cardiology follow-up) | ~100,000 |
| **Total across 3 categories** | **~510,000** |

At current penetration: Ditto has ~1,500 patient MAU = **~0.3% of this addressable population.**

At 50K MAU (EOY target): that would require **~10% penetration of this addressable market.** Highly ambitious for a single product in one country in one year — but not structurally impossible if distribution works.

### Netherlands — Additional context

- **Annual new "life moment" entries:** ~180,000–200,000 people enter one of these three categories per year in NL. This is the annual refresh of the addressable market — roughly 15,000–17,000 new potential Ditto patients entering the funnel every month.
- **The real ceiling question:** Is the NL TAM large enough for 50K MAU? Yes — at 10% penetration of the addressable specialist-care population, or at lower penetration but with care circle multiplier adding non-patient MAU.

### Europe

| Category | EU/European prevalence |
|---|---|
| Cancer (5-year) | ~20 million |
| Neurodegenerative (active) | ~10 million |
| Cardiac heart failure (active) | ~6–7 million |
| **Rough total specialist-care TAM** | **~30–35 million** |

A 0.1% EU penetration = 30,000–35,000 MAU. A 1% penetration = 300,000+ MAU. The international opportunity is structurally enormous relative to the current scale.

## Key implications

**1. The NL TAM is sufficient for the 50K target, but barely.** 10% penetration of the active specialist-care population is ambitious. The question is whether Ditto can grow from 0.3% to 10% penetration in under a year — that requires execution on every lever simultaneously.

**2. Cancer is the right beachhead.** Highest appointment frequency, highest emotional stakes, strongest care circle activation, most motivated to share and involve family. 128,000 new patients/year + 250,000 in active follow-up = a large, well-defined, highly receptive population.

**3. The caregiver angle in neuro is underexplored.** Alzheimer's patients don't record their own appointments — their adult children or partners do. This is potentially a better product fit than the patient-self-records model. The recording party and the summary recipient are the same person, which changes the value proposition completely.

**4. Cardiac is a sleeper segment.** Post-AMI and heart failure patients are highly motivated, have regular specialist contact, and involve family in their care — but Ditto hasn't explicitly targeted this group. No cancer-specific branding required; "understand your cardiology visit" is equally compelling.

**5. International TAM is the real long-term story.** The EU active specialist-care population is ~60× the NL equivalent. Even modest penetration in 2–3 additional countries adds more MAU than dominating NL entirely.
