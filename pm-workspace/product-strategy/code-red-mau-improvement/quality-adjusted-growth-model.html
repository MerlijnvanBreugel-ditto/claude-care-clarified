Quality-Adjusted Growth Model v3-quality

Two-segment inflow: high-intent vs. low-intent users · Extends funnel-leverage-model v2
The core question: Is it better to acquire more users at current quality, or fewer users at higher quality? This model lets you explore the tradeoff by splitting inflow into high-intent (cancer, pregnancy, chronic illness — appointment-ready) and low-intent (broad population — newsletter, NRC, general ads) segments, each with their own activation and retention rates. Compare three strategies: status quo, volume-up, and quality-shift.

Model formula

// Two-segment decomposition (v3-quality)
MAUtotal = MAUhi + MAUlo + MAUCC-passive

// Per-segment steady-state (simplified two-tier retention)
MAUseg = Cseg × (1 + r₁seg / (1 − r₂seg))
Cseg = Inflow × pctseg × P(reg) × actseg × 1/(1 − K)

// Viral coefficient K (blended across segments)
K = (invitehi × Chi + invitelo × Clo) / (Chi + Clo)
    × invites_per_inviter × invite_to_install × invited_act_rate

// Care Circle passive viewers
MAUCC-passive = (MAUhi + MAUlo) × passive_multiplier

// Lifecycle multiplier L (two-tier retention)
Lseg = 1 + r₁seg / (1 − r₂seg)
Known limitations: Steady-state only (no transient dynamics). Segments share the same viral loop parameters (K is blended). Reactivation not modeled — reactivated users are not distinguished by segment. Life Moment mix (cancer vs. neuro vs. cardiac) is collapsed into a single "high-intent" bucket. For per-moment splits, extend C_seg to N sub-segments and use per-moment appointment frequency data.
