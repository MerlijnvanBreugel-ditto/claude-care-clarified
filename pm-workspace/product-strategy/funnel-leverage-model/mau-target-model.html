How it works & what to distrust

// Each projected month: MAU_next = MAU_now × m
m = ρ(retention) + ν(acquisition × registration × activation) × a(virality)

ν = (organic + paid installs) × P(reg) × P(act) ÷ MAU_now   // new actives as a share of MAU
a = 1 / (1 − K)   // viral amplification; explodes as K → 1
ρ = ρ₀ + 0.30 × (M1_retention − 40%)   // effective monthly MAU stickiness
// ρ₀ ≈ 0.92 is reverse-solved so the baseline gives exactly 20%/mo — it is a PLUG, not a measurement.

What this is

  • A decision tool for one question: which lever, moved how far, puts December MAU on 50,000? The answer it surfaces: no single slider does it — you need retention near its ceiling plus roughly double the activated inflow and/or a transformed loop.
  • Acquisition splits into organic and paid. Organic (~115/day = 3,450/mo) is an earned floor that costs nothing and keeps coming. Paid & partner (purple) is bought: a rate you fund every month, gone when spend stops — consumer paid (Meta/Google) is parked today, so this is the lever you'd turn back on. Retention and virality are also "earned" and compound on the whole base; registration and activation scale the inflow. Watch the contribution bars — retention does the most per point but is capped (you cannot retain more than everyone), and virality is near-dead at today's K≈0.10 until you fundamentally rebuild the loop.

What to distrust (read before quoting)

  • ρ is a plug, not a parameter. It is reverse-solved from the observed 20% and silently absorbs reactivation, the loyal tail, and the gap between cohort retention (M1 ≈ 40%) and rolling 30-day MAU persistence (≈ 92%). The retention slider speaks in M1 % — do not read it as ρ.
  • The 20% baseline is a trailing average and decelerating (May was +1.7% week-on-week). The true instantaneous rate is below 20%, so the drift line is optimistic and the gap is, if anything, understated.
  • Rate-based simplification. New-user inflow is held as a constant share of today's MAU, so acquisition reads as a sustained rate lever. In reality bought growth is a one-time level shift you must keep funding. Treat acquisition gains as the least durable on this chart.
  • Point estimates, no uncertainty bands. The retention→ρ slope (0.30) is an assumption, not a fit. Required-rate is highly sensitive to base and horizon: ~39%/mo was the Jan→Dec plan; from June's 2,200 over six months it is ~68%/mo. Five months of drift raised the bar — that is the headline, not the 39%.

Baseline values (Porygon, May 2026)

  • Installs ≈ 7,000/mo = organic ~3,450 (115/day) + paid & partner ~3,550 · Install→reg ≈ 53% · Reg→activated ≈ 15% · M1 next-month retention ≈ 40% · Viral coefficient K ≈ 0.10. Current MAU = 2,200 (June). Jan–Feb actuals are back-estimated at ~20%/mo and flagged.
