# Market Deep Dive

Deeper, focused market research that complements `../market-trend-analysis/`.

- **`market-trend-analysis/`** = the **threat map**: who is coming for Ditto's summary wedge (commoditization, encroachment, regulation, reimbursement, trust). Organized by threat vector.
- **`market-deep-dive/`** (this folder) = the **opportunity-and-mechanics map**: in each layer Ditto could build, who already won that game, what feature made them sticky, how they monetize, and whether the direction is real. Organized to `../long-term-product-vision/own-perspective-v2.md`.

## How to read

1. **`00-scope-and-research-plan.md`** — the spine: scope, complementarity, the 7-lens teardown template, the field + direction structure.
2. **`briefs/`** (F1–F8) — capability-field teardowns: standout global players run through the 7-lens template.
3. **`directions/`** (C1–C6) — syntheses to v2's architecture: Intelligence ladder, PHR multiplier, Operational front door, Coordination lean, Social lean, and the JTBD-forward map.
4. **`synthesis/`** — cross-cutting patterns, the monetization filter, the layer-stack scorecard, and reconciliation with the threat map.
5. **`citation-index.md`** — every claim mapped to a graded source.
