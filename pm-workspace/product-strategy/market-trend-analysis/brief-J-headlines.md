## Resolution notes on the three flagged items

### 1. Oura (the "Aura" the user meant) — VERIFIED, multiple real moves
Oura is making aggressive, real clinical/health-system moves, not a single one:
- **Fullscript partnership** (announced JPM week, Jan 2026): Oura biometrics flow into Fullscript's clinical workflow; by Q2 2026, 125,000+ providers and ~30 health systems can view opt-in Oura sleep/readiness/activity data. Source: https://medcitynews.com/2026/01/fullscript-oura-partnership/
- **Lumeris partnership**: Oura entering primary-care delivery. Source: https://ouraring.com/blog/oura-joins-lumeris/
- Also confirmed: partnerships with **Maven Clinic** and **Midi Health** (women's health virtual care), and an **FDA-track blood-pressure validation study** via Oura Labs (Ring 5, unveiled May 28, 2026). Sources: https://medcitynews.com/2025/11/oura-wearable-healthcare/ and https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260528686853/en/URA-Introduces-The-Worlds-Smallest-Smart-Ring-Oura-Ring-5
- Context stat from MedCity: roughly half of Oura's 5.5M users already use the ring to help manage a chronic condition. Used row 4 (Fullscript) as the lead headline because it's the clearest "wearable → clinical" data point with a hard number.

### 2. "Google Health is launching Kin as a direct competitor" — FALSE as stated. Corrected below.
There is **no Google product called "Kin."** The claim conflates two separate things:
- **Kin Health** is an independent startup, not Google. Founded by practicing-physician brothers **Arpan Parikh (CEO) and Amit Parikh**, plus Kyle Alwyn (technical founder of HeyDoctor). It raised a **$9M seed led by Maveron** (announced ~May 18, 2026), with Town Hall Ventures, Eniac, Pear VC, GoodRx founders, and 30+ physicians participating. Its free app records doctor visits, produces plain-language summaries patients can act on and share, and builds a patient-owned longitudinal record. This is the genuine direct competitor to Ditto. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/kin-health-raises-9m-to-build-an-ai-notetaker-for-patients/ and the Maveron announcement https://maveronvc.substack.com/p/maveron-leads-9m-seed-round-in-kin
- **Google** separately launched **"Google Health Coach"** (a Gemini-powered fitness/sleep/wellness subscription, $9.99/mo, launched May 19, 2026 — the rebranded Fitbit Premium), and Google DeepMind is developing an **"AI Co-clinician."** Neither is named "Kin," and neither is a visit-summary/care-circle product. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/googles-9-99-per-month-ai-health-coach-launches-may-19/
- **Bottom line for the slide:** do NOT say "Google is launching Kin." The accurate, more useful framing is two distinct threats — (a) Kin Health, a Maveron-backed startup doing exactly Ditto's job, and (b) Google going consumer-direct in health AI via a Gemini coach. Both are captured as separate rows (1 and 7).

### 3. ChatGPT health-question volume — VERIFIED, strong primary-ish source
OpenAI's own usage research (reported January 2026) gives the headline stat: **more than 40 million people ask ChatGPT health questions daily**, and **1 in 4 of ChatGPT's ~800M weekly users submit a health prompt each week**; **>5% of all ChatGPT messages globally are about healthcare**, with ~70% of health conversations occurring outside clinical hours. Source: https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/40-million-use-chatgpt-health-questions-openai/808861/
Corroborating, independent data point (Rock Health 2025 Consumer Adoption Survey): **32% of consumers now use an AI chatbot for health information, double the 16% a year earlier.** Source: https://hitconsultant.net/2026/03/23/rock-health-2025-survey-consumer-ai-adoption-chatgpt-healthcare/
Both are usable; the OpenAI figure is the punchier slide stat, the Rock Health one is the "doubling" trend stat.
