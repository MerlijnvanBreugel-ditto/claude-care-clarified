## What changed from v1 (intellectual honesty)

Phase 2 read primary sources and corrected three v1 claims:
1. **DiGA does NOT require Class IIa.** 95% of German DiGA + all French PECAN are Class I. Reimbursement is compatible with staying Class I / outside AI-Act high-risk. The real wall is a **clinical RCT**, not device class. v1's "classification cliff = reimbursement gate" was wrong; the two are **decoupled**.
2. **Epic's patient-direct threat is 2026–27, not today.** Epic in NL is clinician-mediated drafts; "Ask Emmie" was unveiled at HIMSS ~Mar 2026 with no confirmed EU deployment. DAX/Dragon Copilot went GA in NL/BE (2026-02-12) but **clinician-only, no patient summary**. v1 (via Brief E) overstated this.
3. **Mika is cautionary, not a clean precedent.** It never reached permanent DiGA, withdrew twice, and was sold. No live permanent oncology-companion DiGA exists today.

## The one thing

The patient-friendly summary is commoditizing from every direction, and the institution will ship it for free in NL/BE around **2027–28**. The defensible ground is the **patient-owned, family-shareable, cross-provider, consent-first relationship** — a **network moat, not a feature moat**. The strategic imperative is to build that relationship/family loop **before the artifact commoditizes**. The recorded visit is the wedge, not the product.

## Demand is validated (Stream D)

Literacy/recall crisis (patients forget 40–80% of a visit immediately; ~$236B/yr), 63M US caregivers (+45%/decade; NL 1.9M at parity), 76% with ≥1 chronic condition, consumer health-AI use 16%→32% in a year (EU 12–24mo behind), and 31–38% of summary-engagers already share. Every vector points at the same user, moment, and problem. **NL/EU is a real beachhead, not a compromise.**

## The relationship / family moat (Phase 2-C) — the core

**Reframe:** Ditto is a patient + family relationship company; the recorded visit is the wedge. The family loop and the growth engine are the same thing.

**Winning the patient (direct):** condition-specific entry (oncology fits), trusted-referrer hand-off (clinician / pharmacy / charity), local-language long-tail SEO. **Paid installs are the documented failure mode.** Retention comes from a self-monitoring *habit* (~80% vs ~60% at 40 weeks) and human/peer support — the one-shot summary has zero recurrence (this is the live retention problem).

**Winning the family — five compounding mechanics (each with a proof product):**
1. Free for invitees (Jointly / CircleCare)
2. Differentiated Manager / Viewer / Informed roles (**Kin already ships this**)
3. Value-per-member that rises with each join (Lotsa task feed, CaringBridge)
4. The diagnosis trigger as the activation moment
5. The update/summary as the viral unit

**Failure lessons:** stranger-communities get acquired (PatientsLikeMe → UnitedHealth); trackers without a family loop get absorbed (Vinehealth → Sciensus).

**Competitors for the relationship layer:** fragmented but contested. **Kin Health** (most direct, US-only). **Mika / Outcomes4Me** (EU cancer-relationship entrant via Germany — and the same name as the reimbursement story). CaringBridge, Lotsa, Belong, Patronus adjacent.

## The money (Phase 2-A, corrected)

- **Reimbursement is achievable at Class I.** The barrier is a clinical RCT, not certification.
- **Path 1 — stay light (Class I, patient-owned), monetize at home:** four Dutch B2B channels (DBC-embedded, facultatieve prestatie, the **€2.8B IZA transformation funds now committed**, supplementary insurance) **plus the new "diga.nl" provisional-reimbursement scheme** (VWS recommended Dec 2025). Class-I-friendly, home-market, near-term.
- **Path 2 — go clinical (CE Class I + RCT → DiGA):** est. **€1.5M–€3.5M over 2–3 years**; revenue ~€227 / 90 days after a ~59% negotiation haircut. Poor oncology base rate (Mika, CANKADO).
- **These are sequential, not exclusive:** light-now (validate Dutch B2B + diga.nl with real money) → clinical-later (set an explicit go/no-go gate).
- **Three decisions Ditto owes itself:** (1) ratify the intended-purpose statement (controls classification); (2) validate the Dutch B2B route with real procurement money + apply to diga.nl; (3) set the clinical RCT go/no-go gate.
- Caveat: the "€6–12/mo Menzis/Hello24-7" benchmark could **not** be verified — treat as an unverified planning anchor; the real Dutch model is a per-pathway/annual hospital-insurer licence.

## The rules (Stream A, corrected)

- **Near-term, cheap, mandatory: AI Act Article 50 transparency, 2 Aug 2026** — patient-facing AI must perceivably disclose it is AI. Ship the disclosure UX now.
- **Classification cliff is about AI-Act *burden*, not reimbursement.** Explain/organise = Class I. Interpret/flag/triage/recommend = Class IIa + AI-Act high-risk. The "care intelligence layer" ambition is the exact tipping point — decide intended-purpose deliberately.
- You can't outsource AI-Act compliance to your LLM vendor (GPAI → inherit system obligations).
- EHDS (in force Mar 2025) is a **2029+** portability/training-data tailwind. Architecture signal, not 2026 work.

### Two-by-two (the two decision-relevant uncertainties)

Axes: **Artifact commoditization speed** (slow ↔ fast) × **Strength of Ditto's relationship/family moat** (weak ↔ strong).

| | Moat WEAK | Moat STRONG |
|---|---|---|
| Commoditization SLOW | *Borrowed time* — product works a while; race to build the loop. | *Comfortable lead.* |
| Commoditization FAST | *Commoditized & exposed* — summary free everywhere, no lock-in, squeezed out. **(The existential risk.)** | *Win the relationship* — the summary going free is fine; Ditto owns the patient + family habit. **(The target.)** |

**Imperative:** evidence says commoditization is **fast-ish** (portal standard-of-care ~2027–28). So the whole game is moving from the WEAK-moat column to the STRONG-moat column **before 2027–28**. Reimbursement is a parallel, optional revenue/credibility track (home-market diga.nl + B2B tariffs near-term; the RCT-gated DiGA a deliberate, base-rate-risky 2–3yr bet).
