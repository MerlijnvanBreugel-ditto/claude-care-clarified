## The vision (what Care Brain proposes)

Treat the LLM as the **brain of the whole product**, not a summarization endpoint. AI powers every surface (living summary, journey timeline, health profile, circle views, prep, contextual Q&A, nudges) and is invisible: "Ditto just knows," never "I'm using AI." Model-agnostic by design (orchestration routes tasks to the best/cheapest model). 

**The unicorn feature**: *One recording, everyone understands.* Audience-adapted sharing where each circle member gets their own version. Big Tech builds for one person; Ditto builds for the circle.

**The claimed moat (ranked)**: care graph > journey history > bedside trust > regulatory navigation > network density. The thesis: AI is crude oil (commodity); Ditto is the refinery (extraction prompts, audience adaptation, confirmation UX, cross-appointment reasoning, voice, evals). The model is replaceable; the refinery and the graph are not.

**Magic-experience refinement**: the real 2026 moat is **agentic follow-through + longitudinal memory + permission to act**, not extraction quality (which is depreciating). Ship "The Share That Writes Itself" first, not third. Architecture that makes it affordable: pipeline-plugin framework + typed health graph (DB source of truth) + LLM-wiki projection + fact→filter→similarity retrieval + eval-first contract.

**Business case**: EU TAM 80-100M patients + 250-400M circle. Revenue B2C (€7.99/mo) + B2B2C insurer (€0.50-1.00 PMPM) + (later) EHDS data services. AI cost ~$0.07-0.94/user/mo, negligible. Path to €50-100M ARR / unicorn in year 4-5.

## The committed retreat (the one-pager)

In response to the critique, the committed strategy narrows hard: beachhead = **oncology + neurodegenerative patients and their 1-3 loved ones, NL-first** (50% of active users are already oncology). Three JTBDs (hold the story, share without burden, catch what fragmented care misses). Three magic moments (Journey Page, Share That Writes Itself, Cross-Doctor Catch). One PMF proxy (% oncology patients sending ≥1 adapted share/month). A retention commitment (publish the 90-day curve; gate work on it). A moat proof point (≥2 circle members reading weekly, 90 days post adapted-share).

## The critique (Lenny's panel + Merlijn's own notes)

The corpus's loyal opposition is brutal and, on the data, correct:

| Critic | Attack |
|---|---|
| Doshi (LNO) | 80% of the synthesis is well-written overhead. What's the ONE thing this quarter? |
| Cagan | A prescriptive feature roadmap dressed as a vision. Where's the discovery? |
| Dunford | A brand line, not a position. No category, no named competitive alternative (the real one is WhatsApp + a shared doc). |
| Torres | Zero real users. Virtual founder panels are confirmation bias in costume. |
| Balfour | You're building pipes before testing retention. 0.14% daily share is a trickle, not a loop. €7.99 → ~€95 LTV is marginal. 10-person team can't do B2C + B2B2C. |
| Biddle | Delight is proven on paper; Hard-to-copy and Margin are hypotheses. The graph is 4x too sparse to be a network. |

**The five flinches the vision avoids**: patient vs caregiver as customer; compressing 45 JTBDs to 3; B2C vs B2B2C; the retention truth; the summary's kill-date.

## What survives the critique (the real assets)

- The architectural contributions (pipeline-plugin framework, typed graph + wiki projection, eval-first contract) are leverage work, genuinely correct.
- The emotional targeting of the magic moments is good product taste.
- The commoditization threat analysis is honest.
- The reframe "Ditto is a relationship company, the recorded visit is the wedge" is right.

## Implications for the long-term vision

- Take the **architecture and the reframe**; drop the comprehensiveness (45 JTBDs / 22 moments).
- The care-graph moat is the highest-ceiling but least-proven thesis. The long-term vision treats it as **Route E (a bet to validate cheaply)**, not an assumption baked into every route.
- The critique's discipline (one customer, one motion, retention-gated, proof-pointed) is the operating standard for whichever route is chosen.
- "Agentic follow-through + longitudinal memory" is the durable AI moat to carry forward (becomes Route B's spine and a capability layer in A/C).
