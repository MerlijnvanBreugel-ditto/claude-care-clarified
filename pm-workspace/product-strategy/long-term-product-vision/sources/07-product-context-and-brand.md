## What Ditto is today

Ditto Care B.V. (Rotterdam, ~16-18 people, pre-Series A) turns recorded medical conversations into patient-friendly AI summaries patients can reference and share. Stated direction: evolve from a summary tool into the **intelligent care layer for the whole journey**, with the **care circle as the compounding growth loop**.

## Strategy & positioning (current)

Ditto positions between two rejected extremes: a pure social platform (crowded) and a "doctor in your pocket" diagnostic AI (legal risk). Layered evolution: summaries → appointment tracking → care intelligence. Long-term ambition voiced internally: "the digital front door for care in Europe."

Two active exploration threads, both pointing at the frequency problem:
- **"Beyond Appointments"**: appointments are episodic and cap activation; hunt non-visit activation paths.
- **Phone-call recording**: the next major unlock (80% of feature requests, 50% of support tickets).

Post-Code-Red focus narrowed to **high-intensity care paths** (oncology primary, ~50% of active users; cardiology; neurodegenerative/sudden-severe secondary). "Care Brain" is an internal codename only; the team says "care circle" / "care intelligence layer."

## Brand (the voice and the asset to protect)

- **Mission**: "We clarify and connect care journeys for you and your loved ones." Tagline: **Care. Clarified.** Ambition: the European healthcare consumer layer ("the Polarsteps of healthcare").
- **3C proposition**: Capture → Clarify → Connect.
- **Category framing**: the communication layer between patients and the people who care, not another portal, not a health chatbot.
- **Differentiators**: patient-first design, understanding not just access, cross-system integration, customizable care networks, **privacy by architecture** (on-device, never monetized, EU data residency).
- **Competitive framing**: vs Big Tech (they build for individuals; care is multi-person, the defensible asset is the care circle), vs EHR (distribution, no trust), vs insurers (trust, can't ship), vs scribes (integration, no distribution).
- **Voice**: "the sharpest person at the dinner table" — clarity *with* intelligence, warm, confident, trustworthy. Clear ≠ dumbed-down. No single em-dashes, no corporate speak.

## Care Circle (the existing loop)

Shipped Nov 2025. Validated demand: 30% of summaries were already shared via WhatsApp/Signal before the feature existed. Patient shares specific events via invite links → followers view in-app, patient keeps granular privacy control. v2 roadmap: follower groups, reactions/comments, auto-share, link management. Stated future: "visualizing healthcare journeys" (Polarsteps for healthcare). Competitive edge over WhatsApp/MyChart/CaringBridge/Carenzorgt: AI clarity + granular privacy + mobile-first + EU standards.

## Implications for the long-term vision

- Any route must respect the **North Star (Core MAU)** and the near-term Series A milestone, but the vision is explicitly longer-horizon than the June sprint.
- The brand already claims the relationship/consumer-layer position ("Polarsteps of healthcare," care circle as the asset). Routes A and E are most on-brand; B and C extend it; D stretches it toward infrastructure.
- **Privacy-by-architecture and EU-native trust** are existing brand assets that map directly onto the market's trust vacancy (OpenEvidence exit) and Route D's thesis.
- The team's own "Beyond Appointments" and phone-call-recording threads are the frequency problem stated in the team's language; the vision's frequency engines should connect to them.
- Keep the team's real vocabulary ("care circle," "care intelligence layer") in anything shared; "Care Brain" and "Life Moments" are internal-only.
