A vision's shape is read by balance, not size. The blue upper half is what is good — company value (monetisation, excitement) and user value (agency, connection, convenience). The red lower half is what is hard — competition (commoditisation, incumbent reach) and context (regulation, healthcare dependency). A top-heavy blue shape is a winner; one that sinks into the red is fighting gravity. Hover the ghost outline for today's product.

How to read it

The net (blue value minus red challenge) is a crude scalar — the shape matters more. On these draft scores, Social (2) and Coordination (3) sit cleanest: real value, light red. Trusted Health Assistant (5) has the largest blue reach and the deepest red dip — regulation pulls it down hard; the prize and the trap in one shape. Care Companion (1) is dragged by commoditisation. Patient-Owned Record (4) is the only net-negative — thin value, heavy dependency — which is why it reads as substrate, not a destination. And today's product (ghost) is shallow on top and spiked into commoditisation: useful, but exposed.

Scores are research-informed gut (sources 11–14), 1–5 per axis, for recalibration at founder alignment — not measured fact. Edit the VISIONS / AXES blocks at the top of the script and the map redraws. Positive axes are scored so higher = better; challenge axes so higher = more of that problem.
