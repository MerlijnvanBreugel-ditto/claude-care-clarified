# Long-Term Product Vision

Forward-looking synthesis of where Ditto's product, strategy, and monetization can go beyond the current summary product. Built 2026-06-03 by Mewtwo with Merlijn.

## What's here

- **[`decision-canvas.md`](./decision-canvas.md)** — the working front door: 2031 vision, routes table (claim / in / out), five challenges, proposed decisions, eigenquestions, answering plan, coming weeks. Built for the product strategy session. Start here.
- **[`long-term-product-vision.md`](./long-term-product-vision.md)** — the synthesis and the bet. Five product × strategy × monetization routes, a comparison, and a recommendation (an A+C "caregiver in the treatment window" hybrid).
- **[`sources/`](./sources/)** — one distilled artifact per evidence base the vision rests on. Each is a synthesis, **not a copy**, and links back to the live originals elsewhere in the repo.

## Why this exists

The current product (recorded visit → patient-friendly summary) is commoditizing, low-frequency, and leans on reimbursement to monetize. This package reframes the strategy question around the two binding constraints (frequency and reimbursement-independent monetization) and lays out distinct ways to build something durable, AI-native, high-need, and fast to scale.

## Status

Exploratory. Not yet a committed strategy. The current committed artifact is [`specs/care-brain/care-brain-one-pager.md`](../../specs/care-brain/care-brain-one-pager.md). Once a route is chosen, this should either supersede that one-pager or be merged into it.
