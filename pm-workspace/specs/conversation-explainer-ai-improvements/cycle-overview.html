What's in the new prompts · per-node thinking levels are the lever

8 new Langfuse prompts at production/conversation/Gemini/3.5_flash/<step> v1. Trimmed 40 to 68% versus the prior Gemini prompts: no chain-of-thought scaffolding (thinking is native), no redundant allow/deny lists, no JSON schema repetition. Variables and Pydantic contracts preserved exactly.

01 classificationMINIMAL
02 medical extractionMEDIUM
04 action extractionMEDIUM
05 summary.coreHIGH
05 titleMINIMAL
06 qualitycheckMEDIUM
06 error_extractionbudget 8192
06 rewritingHIGH

Key finding: latency on Gemini 3.5 Flash is dominated by thinking-token generation (about 85 to 90% of per-node latency), not by input length. Token/latency correlation across iterations was 0.93 to 0.99 with thinking tokens, only 0.07 to 0.73 with input tokens. Prompt-trim wins are real but small. Per-node thinking is the high-leverage lever.

  • HIGH on summary.core and rewriting. Dropping to MEDIUM did not save latency but cost ~0.034 understandability. Kept at HIGH.
  • budget=8192 on error_extraction. Uncapped HIGH caused outliers up to 940s (3 × 300s timeout cascades). Median HIGH usage was ~6000 tokens, so 8192 bounds the tail without hurting typical quality.
  • MINIMAL on classification and title. No reasoning needed. 3 to 4s saved per node.
  • Single-provider chain, permissive circuit breaker. Strict breaker thresholds caused full-dataset cascade failure in v1.

Iteration history · v1 → v5c, five days

Five iterations took the pipeline from total cascade failure to shadow-ready parity with V2. The valuable insight is what each iteration disproved.

Iter Date Change Pass Headline
v12026-05-19 All-Gemini chain, 60s timeout, strict circuit breaker 0/100 HIGH-thinking timeout cascaded via strict breaker. Every subsequent item hit "all circuit breakers open".
v22026-05-19 300s timeout, permissive breaker, HIGH on heavy nodes 52/100 Langfuse stalled at 52. Best quality observed so far. Becomes the in-experiment baseline.
v32026-05-20 HIGH → MEDIUM on heavy nodes; LOW on qualitycheck 100/100 Understandability dropped 0.034 vs v2. Latency on summary/rewriting did not improve. Thinking-level dropping is non-linear.
v42026-05-20 budget=8192 on 3 heavy nodes; 120s timeout 90/100 p50 −12s vs v2 paired. Still 0.034 down on understandability. 120s was too tight a timeout.
v5c2026-05-20 HIGH on summary+rewriting; budget=8192 on error_extr; 180s timeout 93/100 Shadow-ready. Quality recovered to V2 parity within noise. p50 −8.3s vs V2 paired. Mean −14.4s vs v4.

Known limitations · from the manifest card

  • No fallback in the experiment manifest. If Vertex EU is unavailable, the pipeline hard-fails. Acceptable for shadow, not served. The live A/B wrapper (PR #316) still keeps Azure-V2 as primary, so user-facing traffic is safe.
  • Outlier items (3 to 5%) still hit the 180s timeout on the patient-language nodes. End up as guardrail-style 400 responses, not bad outputs.
  • Token uncertainty is unavailable on Gemini (no logprobs). The token_uncertainty guardrail returns 0.0 for this pipeline. Already true in V2's Gemini fallback path. PR #316 added score=None semantics so this no longer contaminates comparisons with fake zeros.
  • Iteration validation used the Direct API (api_key); production manifest now points at Vertex. The validated artifact is the prompt + thinking-level config, which is provider-independent, but smoke-test on Vertex before shadow → served.
