Two Problems, One Root Cause

The activation funnel leaks through three holes, each needing a different fix.

Acquisition Mismatch

Performance marketing acquires too broad a group. Many users are "too healthy" — they're not in a life moment window, so Ditto doesn't feel urgent or relevant.

Activation Dependency

Even for the right users, the aha moment requires an external event (a doctor appointment) that Ditto doesn't control. No appointment = no value = no retention.

The Flo Analogy

Flo started as a period tracker, not a "women's health platform." They nailed one life moment (menstrual cycle), built trust, then expanded to pregnancy, fertility, menopause. Each expansion was natural because trust was already earned. Ditto should do the same: own cancer, then expand.

Four Layers of Patient Need

Most health tech only addresses Layer 1. Ditto's opportunity is going deeper. Based on psychosocial research on cancer and serious illness.

Click each layer for evidence and Ditto's role

Layer 1 — Informational (surface)

I need to know what the doctor said

What did they say about the scan results? Was it stage 2 or 3?

Table stakes for any health information tool. Necessary but not differentiating on its own.

Ditto today: AI-powered summaries in plain language. This is where the product already delivers.
Layer 2 — Psychological

I need to feel less anxious, less overwhelmed, more in control

I don't even know what questions to ask. I feel like things are happening TO me.

58% of cancer patients feel their emotional needs get less attention than physical needs. Strengthening Sense of Coherence directly improves resilience, self-efficacy, and quality of life.

35-80% of cancer patients experience significant psychological distress — not just about the disease, but about not understanding and not knowing what comes next.

Clarity: Comprehensibility — plain language, explained terms, "here's what this means for you"
Convenience: Manageability — everything organized, prepared for next appointment, trusted resources at hand
Layer 3 — Relational

I need to not be alone — but I don't want to be a burden

My daughter calls every day asking how I am. I love it and I hate it. I'm exhausted from retelling.

The most painful tension in serious illness. Caregivers suffer from information deficits, helplessness, and psychological strain — not because they don't care, but because they don't know enough to help effectively.

The patient sees this and feels guilty. Research frames this as existential isolation: the awareness of facing this alone, in tension with a deep need for belonging.

Connection: Share without retelling. Circle can act without asking. Supportive messages flow naturally. The burden lifts from the patient without them having to manage it.
Layer 4 — Existential (deepest)

Who am I now? Do I still matter? Am I still me?

I used to be a teacher, a mother, a friend. Now I'm just "a cancer patient."

Two core drivers of existential distress: self-discontinuity ("I don't recognize my life") and loss of personal autonomy ("I can't make my own choices").

Patients report a desire to affirm aspects of their lives unrelated to their illness — strengths, roles, ability to contribute. Most health tech treats the patient as a data subject.

Ditto's opportunity: the patient is the author of their care story, not a passive recipient. They curate, they share on their terms, they decide. Ditto preserves agency in a system that takes it away.

Three Workstreams

Each phase feeds the next. Clarity creates something worth coming back to. Convenience creates the habit. Connection creates the growth loop.

1 Now (weeks)

Fix the funnel for cancer patients

Diagnose and remove specific blockers. Right people, still don't activate — why?

Clarity
  • Customer.io + Typeform: survey drop-offs to understand WHY
  • Mixpanel: funnel analysis segmented by user type
  • Recording permission support (reduce fear barrier)
  • Calendar integration with deeplink to start recording
  • Intent question in onboarding (BIGB-2190)
2 Next (months)

Deepen the cancer journey

Beyond recording: give reasons to open Ditto BETWEEN appointments. Build the habit layer.

Convenience Clarity
  • "Speak to Ditto" — voice health overview (bridge-to-value)
  • "Understand your letter" — document explanation at home
  • Smart appointment questions (pre- and end-of-conversation)
  • Journey overview — visualize care across providers and decisions
  • Symptom tracking between appointments
3 Parallel with CC v2

Make sharing effortless and valuable

Care Circle as distribution + emotional support layer. Turn every active patient into 2-5 activated circle members.

Connection
  • Effortless sharing (one-tap, automatic circle updates)
  • Follower-first aha (see a real summary before installing)
  • Supportive messages (heartwarming, practical help offers)
  • Care Circle timing A/B test (appointment-first vs circle-first)
  • Caregiver-specific features for cognitive decline life moment
