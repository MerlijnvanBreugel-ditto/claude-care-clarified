The Insight

It's a care circle,
not a chatbot.

The defensible asset is not the AI. It's the Care Circle —

The Care Circle

Different people need different things.

The partner
Full summary, action items, medication changes, emotional context
The adult child
Key decisions, next appointments, how parent is feeling
The elderly parent
Simplified update, reassurance, what matters most
The friend abroad
High-level status, what would actually help

Not "share one post to everyone." The right information,
to the right person, in the right format.
