# Mobbin Research: Care Circle Pulse UI Inspiration

**Date**: 2026-02-02
**Research Focus**: Social health apps, caregiver coordination, daily status sharing, support gestures

---

## 1. Daily Status Sharing Patterns

### Apps Analyzed for Status Check-in

| App | Key Pattern | Inspiration For |
|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| **BeReal** | Daily prompt, quick capture | Direction B: Time-limited check-in |
| **Daylio** | Mood tracking with icons | Direction A: Status selection |
| **Bearable** | Health symptom tracking | Direction A: Structured check-in |
| **Marco Polo** | Async video messages for families | Direction B: Social engagement |
| **Life360** | Family location/status sharing | Direction A: Caregiver visibility |
| **Headspace** | Mood check post-meditation | Direction C: Mindful approach |

### Common Patterns Observed

**Status Selection Patterns:**
1. **Emoji-based**: Happy/neutral/sad faces (Daylio)
2. **Slider-based**: 1-10 scale with anchors
3. **Word-based**: "Great", "Okay", "Struggling"
4. **Visual metaphor**: Weather, energy levels, colors

**Daily Prompt Patterns:**
1. **Time-sensitive**: BeReal's random notification
2. **Routine-based**: Morning/evening check-ins
3. **Event-triggered**: After medication, appointment
4. **Optional**: Available but not pushed

### Life360 Patterns
- Map-based family visibility
- "Check-in" button for status
- Crash detection alerts
- Message/call shortcuts

### Caring Village
- Shared calendar
- Task assignment
- Medication tracking
- Document storage

### CaringBridge
- Health journal updates
- Guestbook for support
- Photo sharing
- Privacy controls

### Relevant for Ditto
- Status should be passive (visible without explicit sharing)
- Exception alerts (only notify when concerning)
- Support gestures (low-friction reactions)

## 3. Support Gesture Patterns (Direction B/C Inspiration)

### iMessage Reactions
- Tapback on messages (heart, thumbs up, etc.)
- Non-verbal acknowledgment
- Doesn't require response

### Facebook Care Reaction
- Heart + hands emoji
- Specific to supportive content
- Normalized during pandemic

### Relevant for Ditto
- Hearts, flowers, hugs are universal support symbols
- Voice messages add personal touch
- "Offer to help" (cooking, transport) is specific and actionable
- Reactions should feel meaningful, not automated

## 4. Wellness-Focused Status Apps (Direction C Inspiration)

### Headspace Mood Tracking
- Pre/post session mood logging
- Simple face selection
- No judgment, just awareness
- Data used for personalization

### Calm Daily Check-in
- Mood as part of daily routine
- Connected to content recommendations
- Gentle, not clinical

### How We Feel (Apple partnership)

## 5. Synthesis: Three Design Directions

### Direction A: On-Brand (Ditto-Native)
**Inspiration**: Daylio + Life360
- Standard Ditto design system
- Three-option status buttons
- Card-based caregiver feed
- Tab bar visible
- Practical, clear

### Direction B: Partly On-Brand (Social)
**Inspiration**: BeReal + iMessage reactions
- Ditto colors preserved
- More visual/animated status options
- Social feed style for caregivers
- Reaction system for support
- Streak/engagement tracking

### Direction C: Experimental (Mindful)
**Inspiration**: Headspace + How We Feel
- Full-screen immersive
- Orb-based status selection
- Floating gesture bubbles
- Warm gradients
- Connection as ritual

---

## 6. Key UX Considerations

### For Patients
- Status sharing must feel voluntary, not surveilled
- Voice notes add nuance without typing
- "Need support" shouldn't feel like failure
- Should take <10 seconds

### For Caregivers
- Default to "all is well" unless otherwise
- Exception-based alerts reduce anxiety
- Easy support gestures create connection
- Don't require monitoring behavior

### Privacy Considerations
- Inner circle (partner) vs outer circle (friends)
- Status visibility controls
- Voice notes optional to share
- Transparent about who sees what
