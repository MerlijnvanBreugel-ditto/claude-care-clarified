Smart Suggestions

The brain analyzed Mara's oncology conversation and generated 3 contextual questions she'd likely want answered. Not generic. Specific to THIS appointment.

Suggestions are pre-computed during the recording pipeline, so they appear instantly when Mara views the summary.

Why this matters

The patient doesn't need to know how to "use AI." They don't type a prompt. The brain suggests, they tap. Guided intelligence.

Architecture

Grounded Answer

The answer is generated by answer_worker (Claude Sonnet, streaming, first token ~1.5s). It uses ONLY data from Mara's conversation transcript + her health profile. Never general medical knowledge.

suggestion.tapped
  → Load context: transcript + summary + profile
  → answer_worker (Sonnet, streaming)
  → Answer cached for instant replay

Grounding rules

Every answer cites the doctor: "Dr. van der Berg legde uit dat..." If the question can't be answered from the conversation, the brain says so explicitly.

User flow

Ask Something Else

For the minority of users who want more than the suggested questions, there's a text input. Same grounding rules apply. Rate-limited to 5 custom questions per summary.

This is Phase 2 (ships after suggestion chips are validated). Most users will never need it. The suggestions cover 80% of questions.

Tracking

Custom questions are stored for analytics: what are people asking that we didn't suggest? This feeds back into better suggestion generation.

Problem

No single source of truth

Patients can't remember their full medication list, conditions, or care team. This information is scattered across hospital portals, paper letters, and memory.

Health apps that require manual profile entry have near-zero completion rates for 50+ audiences.

The brain's approach

The Health Profile Store is auto-populated from every interaction. Every conversation, every document, every voice input enriches it. The patient only corrects, never creates.

Architecture

Living Document

Every field has source attribution: which worker extracted it, from which input, with what confidence. Conflicts are detected (two sources disagree about a medication) and flagged.

This creates massive switching costs. 18 months of auto-extracted health context can't be recreated elsewhere.
