# Brief H: Where the Patient + Family Care Relationship Lives TODAY (Before Ditto)

*Workstream H of the Ditto market/trend scan. Scan date: 2026-05-30. Question: WHERE is the patient/family health relationship fostered today, before Ditto? These venues are the de-facto incumbents for the relationship Ditto wants to own. Most are NOT purpose-built apps.*

> Sources are numbered [n] inline and listed in §8. `[F]` = fact (sourced) · `[I]` = inference · `[E]` = estimate. Scale figures are vendor- or press-reported and directional unless noted. Recency flagged where it matters.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- **The relationship already exists. It is just scattered across four venues Ditto does not own, none of them purpose-built for "understand and coordinate this patient's care."** The de-facto incumbents are (1) patient communities, (2) Facebook/Reddit social groups, (3) WhatsApp family/patient threads, and (4) AI chatbots. Ditto is not creating a new behavior; it is consolidating one that is already happening in worse tools. `[I]`
- **The biggest venue is the most invisible one: WhatsApp.** ~89% of Dutch internet users (16+) are on WhatsApp — the highest-penetration channel in the country, used daily, by the whole family [11]. The "how is mum doing?" thread already runs there. No competitor owns it; no competitor can — but it is also where Ditto's update/share unit must land to be adopted. `[F][I]`
- **AI chat has, in ~24 months, become a mass clarification venue — and crucially, it is used for exactly Ditto's job: explaining the visit.** 40M+ people ask ChatGPT health questions *every day*; 48% of health users use it to "clarify medical terminology or instructions" and 55% to check symptoms [9][13]. 1 in 4 US adults (66M+) used AI for health info in late 2025 [8]. This is the single fastest-growing and most behaviorally aligned venue — and the clearest threat. `[F]`
- **Dedicated patient communities are large but mostly peer-support and increasingly pharma-monetized, not family-coordination.** Inspire: 3M+ members, 10M+ annual visitors [3]. CaringBridge: ~300k daily users, new page every 12 min [1]. In NL, Kanker.nl has a 41,000-member community and ~780k monthly visits [5]. They own "people like me" and "post an update," not "understand today's visit + coordinate my circle." `[F][I]`
- **Facebook health groups are still huge (70M+ users, 6M+ groups as of 2017) [2] but structurally decaying for the next generation** — Gen Z Facebook use fell 87%→77% 2016–25; teen use 71%→33% over the decade [14]. Betting the relationship on Facebook is betting on a shrinking rail. `[F][I]`
- **Strategic read:** the relationship is real, daily, and fragmented across general-purpose tools — which is simultaneously Ditto's biggest opportunity (the consolidation gap is wide open, no one owns "comprehend + coordinate this patient") and its biggest threat (AI chat is racing into the comprehension job from one side; the family thread already lives on WhatsApp on the other). Ditto must out-specialize the chatbots and plug into WhatsApp rather than fight it. `[I]`

## 2. Patient communities / dedicated platforms (table)

These are the closest things to purpose-built venues. Most serve *peer support* or *care logistics* — not "understand this specific visit." Scale figures are vendor/press-reported.

| Platform | Role / relationship it serves | Scale (date) | Read for Ditto |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inspire** (US) [3] | World's largest health community; condition-specific peer support; monetizes via pharma/life-sciences real-world insights. | **3M+ members across 3,000+ communities; 10M+ visitors/year; 1M members reached Feb 2017.** | Owns "people like me" + drug/treatment Q&A. Patient-as-data-product model. Not family coordination, not visit comprehension. |
| **CaringBridge** (US-origin, 240+ countries) [1] | Free health-journey journal + care coordination; "no one goes through a health crisis alone." | **~300k daily users; new page every 12 min; ~1,600 messages/hour; ~1.7M monthly users (traffic est.).** Longitudinal study (609 authors, ~82 days): ~30% felt significantly less overwhelmed [1]. | Owns the *emotional update* + the share-with-circle behavior. Closest spiritual analog to Ditto's circle. No AI, no visit comprehension, no cross-provider record. |
| **Lotsa Helping Hands / MyCancerCircle** (US) [6] | Care-coordination calendar (meals, rides, tasks) around a caregiver; MyCancerCircle is the cancer-branded instance (w/ CancerCare). | Long-established; "60 seconds to create a community"; no public total. | Owns logistics: shared calendar + task feed = value-per-member. Mechanic to copy. Not comprehension. |
| **Smart Patients** (US) [12] | Online community for cancer patients + caregivers; learn treatments, find trials, share practice. Founded by Gilles Frydman (ACOR) + Roni Zeiger (ex-Google Health). | **100+ communities** across oncology, autoimmune, neuro; caregiver communities built in. | Pedigree + caregiver focus, but a forum, not a coordination/comprehension tool. |
| **PatientsLikeMe** (US) [4] | Pioneer patient-data-sharing network (founded 2006); patients log conditions/treatments; data sold to pharma. | Forced to divest Chinese majority owner iCarbonX (had raised $100M); **acquired by UnitedHealth Group, 2019.** | Cautionary tale: the *data*, not the relationship, was the prize; public network needed an acquirer. Private circle is more defensible. |
| **Belong.Life (Belong Cancer)** (Israel/global) [phase2-C] | "World's largest social cancer network"; AI-personalized community + navigation; caregiver groups; hospital-licensed. | ~200k cancer patients/caregivers (2026 est.). | Owns cancer *community* relationship (strangers), B2B2C licensing. Different relationship than Ditto's private circle. |
| **Kanker.nl** (NL) [5] | Dutch national reliable-cancer-info + lotgenoten (peer) community; run with KWF/IKNL. | **41,000 community members; ~1,850 bloggers; ~780k visits/month; ~50 volunteer moderators (2025).** | The incumbent NL cancer *relationship* venue. Trust + reach, but info + peer support, not visit comprehension or family coordination. Likely partner, not just competitor. |
| **Carenzorgt / Caren (Nedap)** (NL) [7] | Free family-care-coordination + digital care file: shared agenda, task division, invite relatives, view home-care visits + some clinical data (BP, nursing notes). Caren 3.0 rollout Nov 2025. | No public user total; widely deployed via NL care orgs. | Closest NL analog to Ditto's *family-coordination* job — already does shared calendar + invite-circle + clinical-data visibility. Watch closely; tied to professional-care orgs, not patient-direct, no AI comprehension. |
| **Stupid Cancer** (US) [phase2-C-class] | Young-adult cancer community/events/advocacy. | Niche, AYA-focused; no large platform scale. | Community/brand, not a coordination platform. |

**Read:** dedicated platforms split into two jobs — *peer support* (Inspire, Smart Patients, Belong, Kanker.nl) and *care logistics* (Lotsa, Carenzorgt). **None combines "comprehend this specific visit" with "coordinate my private family circle."** That intersection is the open lane. The NL-specific incumbents (Kanker.nl for trust/peer, Carenzorgt for coordination) are the ones to study and likely partner with rather than displace. `[I]`

## 3. Social / open communities (Facebook, Reddit)

The relationship also lives in general-purpose social platforms repurposed for health.

- **Facebook is the largest single host of disease communities — but it is decaying for the next generation.** As of 2017 a Facebook exec reported **70M+ users in 6M+ health-condition groups** [2]. Academic mapping found **6,398 support groups covering 826 diseases**; ~two-thirds are private (privacy need); content is **53% interpersonal support, 31% information sharing** [2a]. **Contrarian flag:** Gen Z Facebook use fell **87%→77% (2016–25)**, US teen use **71%→33%** over the decade; ~32% of 18–24s say they've significantly cut usage [14]. Groups remain Facebook's stickiest feature, but the demographic base for *new* patients is eroding — a relationship rail you don't want to bet a decade on. `[F][I]`
- **Reddit is a fast-growing health Q&A and peer venue.** It hosts **700+ health communities**; r/AskDocs alone has **~789k members** and was one of the most-posted health subreddits in a 2025 analysis (~12% of health posts studied) [10]. Reddit reports health/wellness content views **up 61% in two years** [10]. Cancer subreddits are "rapidly growing communities," though some common cancers (renal, liver, stomach, endometrial) are unrepresented [10a]. `[F]`
- **Behavioral point:** people bring *the same job* to these venues that Ditto targets — "what does this result/term mean," "what should I ask," "has anyone been through this." But the answers come from strangers and crowds, with misinformation risk, no privacy guarantee, and no tie to *your* record or *your* family. `[I]`

**Read:** social communities prove enormous latent demand for health understanding + peer reassurance, but they are public, generational-risk-exposed (Facebook), and unconnected to the individual's actual care. Ditto's private, record-grounded, family-scoped model is the structural answer to their weaknesses — not a clone of them. `[I]`

## 4. Messaging: WhatsApp family/patient groups (the dominant informal channel)

This is the largest and most overlooked venue — the place the family relationship *actually* runs day to day.

- **WhatsApp is the default communication layer in NL and much of the EU.** ~**89% of Dutch internet users (16+)** use WhatsApp — the country's most-used platform; other measures put penetration as high as ~93% [11]. The family group chat is already universal infrastructure. `[F]`
- **Families and patients already use it for health coordination and clarification — it's just informal and undocumented.** Evidence base is qualitative/sectoral rather than a single clean prevalence number:
  - In one parent/caregiver health-communication study, **25.2% received health-education messages via WhatsApp** (2nd only to Facebook at 31.9%) [11a]. `[F]`
  - Among Israeli hospital staff, professional WhatsApp use was near-universal (**100% of physicians, 97.4% of nurses**) — it "improved communication among caregivers" and was "preferred due to broad adoption" [11b]. `[F]`
  - An oncology narrative review found WhatsApp "widely prevalent among health professionals, cancer patients, caregivers and the general population," facilitating doubt-resolution between patients/relatives and the care team via text and voice notes [11c]. `[F]`
  - NL/EU caregiving-coordination research repeatedly identifies informal digital messaging as how care networks "stay connected," while finding formal-informal communication generally *low* and *fragmented* — i.e., the need is met poorly by ad-hoc chat [11d][11e]. `[F]`
- **Caveat / gap:** there is no single high-quality survey quantifying "% of NL families that run a WhatsApp group for a sick relative's care." The claim that WhatsApp is the dominant informal channel rests on (a) near-universal penetration + (b) convergent qualitative evidence, not one headline statistic. `[E][I]`

**Read:** WhatsApp owns the *channel* but not the *job* — it has no structure, no record, no roles, no comprehension layer, and it loses everything in scroll. This is the most important strategic fact in the brief: the relationship's home address is a chat thread, and the chat thread is bad at care. Ditto should treat WhatsApp as the distribution rail (the share/invite of a visit summary must land natively in a WhatsApp thread) rather than as a competitor to replace. `[I][R]`

## 5. AI assistants as the clarification venue

This overlaps the AI brief, but here the point is specific: **the chatbot has become the venue patients already use to clarify their care — Ditto's exact job.**

- **Scale, and growth, are extraordinary.** OpenAI (Jan 2026): **40M+ people ask ChatGPT healthcare questions every day**; health is **>5% of all ChatGPT messages globally** (billions/week); ~**1.5–2M health-insurance questions/week**; **~7 in 10 health conversations happen outside clinical hours** [9][13]. `[F]`
- **The usage is behaviorally identical to Ditto's wedge.** Of health users (Dec 2024 breakdown): **55% checked/explored symptoms, 48% sought clarification of medical terminology or instructions, 40%+ explored treatment options** [13]. "Clarify medical terminology or instructions" *is* the after-visit comprehension job. `[F]`
- **Mass adoption, confirmed by independent polling.** West Health–Gallup (survey Oct–Dec 2025, 5,500+ US adults): **1 in 4 US adults (~66M+) used AI for health info/advice**; most use it to *supplement* a doctor visit (before/after), not replace [8]. By generation: ~**1 in 4 under-30s** vs **~1 in 7 over-50s** [8a]. `[F]`
- **People are actively uploading their own medical data.** Documented behavior (NPR/KFF, Sep 2025): patients screenshot MyChart lab results and upload them to ChatGPT to interpret them and prep questions before appointments [8a]. OpenAI/Apple are formalizing this — ChatGPT Health now links patient portals directly [brief-E]. `[F]`
- **Contrarian / quality + trust caveats (Ditto's wedge):** trust is *lower* for medical advice than other domains — only ~35% trust ChatGPT for medical advice; ~64% think it shouldn't give medical/legal advice; **56% (2024 KFF)** aren't confident AI health info is accurate [9b]. Research shows chatbots over/under-emphasize findings and sometimes leave patients *more* confused; general-purpose ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude are **not HIPAA-compliant** and data flows to the model vendor [8a]. `[F]`

**Read:** AI chat is the fastest-growing and most behaviorally-aligned venue for Ditto's job — and therefore the most direct threat *and* the strongest proof of demand. But it has three structural holes Ditto fills: (1) it's not grounded in *your* actual visit/record (hallucination, generic answers), (2) it's a solo experience with no family circle, (3) it's not privacy-/GDPR-safe. Ditto = the trusted, record-grounded, family-shareable version of the thing 40M people already do daily. `[I][R]`

## 6. Synthesis: the fragmented relationship = Ditto's gap

The patient+family care relationship is fostered today across **four non-purpose-built venues, each owning one slice of the job and none owning the whole**:

| Venue | What it owns | What it lacks (the Ditto gap) | Scale anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI chat (ChatGPT etc.)** | On-demand clarification ("what does this mean?") | Not grounded in your record; solo; not privacy-safe | 40M+ daily health users [9][13] |
| **WhatsApp family threads** | The channel + the daily family relationship | No structure, record, roles, or comprehension | ~89% NL penetration [11] |
| **Patient communities** (Inspire, CaringBridge, Kanker.nl, Carenzorgt) | Peer support + care logistics | Not visit-comprehension; public peers or org-tethered | Inspire 3M+ [3]; Kanker.nl 41k [5] |
| **Facebook / Reddit groups** | Mass peer support + crowd Q&A | Public, generational decline (FB), no record tie | FB 70M+ in health groups [2] |

`[F]/[I] as cited above`

**The implication is a classic consolidation opportunity:**

- **Opportunity (the gap is wide and unclaimed).** No incumbent combines *comprehension of this specific visit* + *a private family circle* + *privacy-by-design*. AI chat does comprehension but solo and ungrounded; WhatsApp does family but unstructured; communities do peers but not your visit. Ditto is the only natural consolidator of "understand + coordinate *this* patient's care, privately, with *this* family." Demand is proven at massive scale — Ditto isn't betting on new behavior, it's organizing existing behavior. `[I]`
- **Threat (the venues are converging on Ditto's job from two sides).** AI chat is racing *into* comprehension (ChatGPT Health linking portals; 40M/day) and Epic/MyChart is adding patient-facing AI summaries [brief-E]. From the other side, the family relationship is so cheap to run on WhatsApp that Ditto must be *meaningfully better than a free chat thread* to earn a place — the bar is "worth leaving the group chat for," not "better than nothing." `[I]`
- **The strategic posture this argues for:** (1) **Out-specialize the chatbots** — be the *grounded, trustworthy, oncology-deep* version of the thing 40M people do daily, leaning on the trust gap (only 35% trust AI for medical advice) and GDPR as the wedge. (2) **Ride WhatsApp, don't fight it** — the share/invite of a visit summary must land natively in the family thread; WhatsApp is the distribution rail, not the rival. (3) **Partner with the NL incumbents** (Kanker.nl for trust/reach, possibly Carenzorgt for coordination) rather than rebuild peer/logistics from scratch. `[R]`

> One-line thesis: **The relationship already runs daily — on WhatsApp, in ChatGPT, in Facebook groups, on Kanker.nl — in tools that each do one-quarter of the job badly. Ditto's opening is to be the first venue purpose-built to do the whole job: comprehend the visit, hold the journey, and coordinate the circle, privately.** `[R]`

AI frustration? clinician?) — this directly validates which venue to consolidate from first.
