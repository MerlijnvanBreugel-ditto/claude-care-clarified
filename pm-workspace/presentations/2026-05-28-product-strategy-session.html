03 / 08
The 3 Life Moments

Cancer, Neurodegenerative, Sudden severe diagnosis.

Together: ~510k NL adults in active specialist follow-up. 50k MAU = 10% penetration of this segment — ambitious but structurally feasible.

Cancer

~250k NL active · ~128k new/year

Highest appointment frequency, highest emotional stakes, strongest care-circle activation pattern. The unambiguous beachhead.

Neurodegenerative

~160k NL active · Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, MS, ALS

Caregiver-led recording matches the Care Circle thesis natively. Strong partner content opportunity (cancer.nl-style resource linking).

Sudden severe diagnosis

~100k+ NL · cardiac, masses, acute findings

Broader than cardiac-only. Post-AMI, new heart failure, AFib, newly-found masses — high specialist contact, family-involving, untargeted today.

Out of primary focus (this period)
Pregnancy / young parents · Sudden chronic disease without severity escalation. Keep on the radar; don't build for them.

Source: pm-workspace/personal/strategy/code-red-mau-improvement/research-life-moments-tam.md (May 2026 TAM research).

04 / 08
Bet 1

A real follower experience.

Today, followers exist only to receive. Tomorrow, they have their own onboarding, their own reason to return, and a way to engage back with the patient.

Followers today
  • Click invite link · accept
  • View summary when patient shares
  • Send ❤
  • Wait
Followers tomorrow
  • Progressive onboarding (relationship · role · what you'll receive)
  • Thoughtful-message sharing (more than ❤)
  • Pre-appointment notification (your person sees the oncologist Thursday)
  • Inbound reactions back to patient (schedule a call · confirm help offer)

Decision today Which lands first — progressive onboarding, thoughtful messaging, or pre-appointment notification? Name an owner.

Bet 2

Drive frequency without inventing a new product.

Most candidates are already on the table — either in flight in Linear or specced in the repo. Pick two, commit, ship.

Candidate Source Why it drives frequency
Pre-appointment Care Circle notification Linear (new) · Niek raised in May 21 Returns followers to the app on a calendar event, not just on patient action.
Thoughtful-message sharing Linear (new) Adds patient ↔ circle moments outside the summary loop. Doesn't depend on a recording.

Care journeys (mobile visualization) O2-KR4 · backlog Living timeline patients and followers return to — anchors frequency even without a new appointment.
Beyond Appointments: Expanding Activation Paths O2-KR2 · backlog Named umbrella project. Needs concrete scope locked this session.
Medical Data Vault O2-KR4 · backlog Between-visit utility (medication, vaccination), self-updating from past recordings.

Decision today Pick 2 of 6 to commit. Each gets an owner and a target shipping window.
