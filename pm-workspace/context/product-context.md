Team: ~10 people | Stage: Early growth | Users: 35,000+

## What We Do

Ditto transforms medical conversations into patient-friendly summaries using AI. Patients record their doctor visits, and Ditto produces clear, understandable summaries they can reference and share with family.

## Core Product

- **Medical conversation recording** → AI-powered transcription and summarization
- **Patient-friendly summaries** → Plain language, structured, actionable
- **Care Circle** → Share summaries with family members (v2 in development)
- **Platform**: Mobile app (iOS & Android)

## Key Partnerships

- **Menzis** (health insurer) — 2 million potential users
- **Juvoly** — Medical software integration
- **UMCG** — Research collaboration

## User Segments

- **Primary**: Patients visiting specialists (often elderly or chronic conditions)
- **Secondary**: Family members / caregivers (Care Circle)
- **Tertiary**: Healthcare providers (indirect benefit through informed patients)

## Competitive Positioning

Ditto is NOT a transcription tool. It's a care companion that makes healthcare understandable and shareable. Differentiation: patient-first design, Care Circle for family involvement, insurer partnerships for distribution.

## Product Principles

1. **Patient-first**: Every feature serves the patient's understanding
2. **Simple > Complete**: Better to be clear about less than confusing about more
3. **Share to care**: Health is a family matter — enable sharing
4. **Trust through transparency**: Medical data requires absolute trust
5. **Validate before building**: Cheap experiments before expensive development
