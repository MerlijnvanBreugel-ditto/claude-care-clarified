## North Star

- **Mission**: We clarify and connect care journeys for you and your loved ones.
- **Tagline**: **Care. Clarified.** (Two words, period-separated, always together. Form mirrors function.)
- **Ambition**: The European healthcare consumer layer. The Polarsteps of healthcare.

## The Problem (evidence anchors)

Healthcare is communicated in the worst possible way: 60% of a consult is forgotten, <25% of medical info is rightly understood, 82% of patients can't update loved ones, +38% 5-year mortality from poor communication. In a world where everything's connected, you can follow your niece's gap year more easily than your mother's care journey.

## The Thesis

The next breakthrough in healthcare won't come from new drugs, data systems, or devices. It comes from giving people the **right information, in the right format, at the right time** — and from treating care as inherently **multi-person**.

## Proposition (3C structure — use as a frame, not a slogan)

- **Capture** — record conversations, upload documents and records (on-device)
- **Clarify** — responsible AI turns medical jargon into understandable text, voice, image
- **Connect** — share the journey safely with the people who care

> "This isn't another medical portal. It's healthcare reimagined — clarity and connection, so you can act, rather than be patient."

## Category & Vision

- **Category language**: the **communication layer** consumers have been missing. Not another patient portal. Not a health chatbot. The social layer between patients and the people who care about them.
- **Consumer-layer analogy**: Google Maps for raw map data, Spotify for music catalogs → Ditto for fragmented healthcare. The interface through which people actually engage with their care.
- **Strategic posture**: "The one who owns the user, owns the patient, owns the opportunities."

## Audience Segments

| Priority | Segment | Core pain | How Ditto helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Older people with increased care needs | Comprehension, scattered info, digital insecurity, fear of burdening loved ones | Unified platform, plain language, simplified interface |
| Secondary | Children, family, caregivers | Fragmented data, messenger burden, emotional load | Permissioned sharing, comprehensive narrative, practical tools |
| Tertiary | People in intense care journeys (oncology, neurodegenerative) | Emotional overload, multi-specialist complexity | Multi-specialist unification, journey tracking, prep |
| Quaternary | People with language barriers | Comprehension, system unfamiliarity, cultural fit | Plain language, visual explanations, translation |

**Top verticals for focus**: onco-hematology (highest fit), neurodegenerative (MS, Parkinson), pregnancy.

## Key Differentiators

1. **Patient-First Design** — built from the person's perspective, not the provider's
2. **Understanding, Not Just Access** — clarity, not another portal of files
3. **Cross-System Integration** — bridges fragmented healthcare data
4. **Customizable Care Networks** — the user defines their circle, not the system
5. **Privacy by Architecture** — on-device, never monetized, "we never hear it"

## Competitive Framing

- **vs Big Tech health AI (Apple, OpenAI, Microsoft, Google)**: they build for individuals; care is fundamentally multi-person. The defensible asset is not the AI — it's the **Care Circle**.
- **vs Legacy EHR (Epic)**: distribution but zero trust, integration, or speed.
- **vs Health insurers**: trust but can't ship.
- **vs Medical scribes**: fast integration, no distribution.
- Ditto sits at the intersection.

## Voice Essentials

**One-line voice**: The sharpest person at the dinner table — makes complex things feel obvious by thinking them through, not by dumbing them down. Warm when it matters. Precise when it counts. Never dull.

### Principles
1. **Clarity with intelligence** — clear ≠ simple. Zero wasted words, but vary rhythm. Earned wit, never puns or wordplay for its own sake.
2. **Human & warm** — "you" and "your"; contractions; rhetorical questions; varied paragraph length. Tone of a thoughtful friend, not a customer service script.
3. **Confident, not corporate** — make claims you can back. Name the problem. No "we strive to" or "we believe."
4. **Trustworthy by default** — specificity is credibility. Name what we DON'T do.

### Use vs Avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Understand, share, summary, your doctor, family / loved ones, Care Circle, recording, private, plain language | Leverage, disseminate, output, healthcare provider, care network (on consumer pages), audio capture, secure (unless architectural), accessible communication, "manage your health" (default), "healthcare moments", "all in one place" |

**Hard avoids**: corporate speak (leverage / facilitate / enable / solution / optimize), filler (very / really / actually / basically / simply), passive reassurance ("we take X seriously"), "Overview" / "Introduction" headers, single em dashes in copy (use period or comma).

### Emotional Territory

Start from the feeling, not the feature. Reference points that have landed:
- Overwhelmed → "Healthcare shouldn't be a puzzle"
- Alone in it → "Because care is something you carry together"
- Confused → "You shouldn't need a medical degree to understand your own health"
- Worried about someone → "When someone you love is dealing with something medical, you want to understand — not just hope for the best"
