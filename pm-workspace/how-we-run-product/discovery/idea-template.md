# Idea Template

Use this template when adding a new idea to the [IDEA Board](https://dittocare.atlassian.net/jira/polaris/projects/BIGB/ideas/view/5417433). Ideas progress through phases — you don't need to fill in everything at once.

This template mirrors the structure used in Jira Product Discovery (BIGB).

---

## Phase 1a: High-Level Describe

> Fill this in when you have an idea and want to describe it in a structured way within 5 minutes.

### Questions to answer:

- **Which problem are we solving, for whom, and why is this important to solve?**
- **What does this feature look like?**
- **What is the expected impact on our North Star metric (MAU)?**
- **What's the cheapest way to validate the expected impact?**
- **Why is this actually a bad idea?** (Goal: find gaps and directions to improve)

If this is an **Exploit** feature rather than Explore, emphasize why it's worth exploiting further and how much your idea improves the existing lever.

---

## Phase 1b: Early Experiment

> Fill this in when you want to validate before committing resources.

### Questions to answer:

- Do we need an experiment/spike to validate feasibility or value?
- What are the goals of that experiment?
- Where are the results documented?
- Which conclusions did we draw?

## Phase 2: Specify

> Required before a feature is put on the roadmap.

### 1. What

**Context**
Describe the situation, trigger, or insight that sparked this idea.

**Problem We Solve**
Clear, crisp problem statement.

**For Whom (User Profile)**
Primary user segment(s) impacted.

**Feature Description**
How do you solve it?

**How to Specify the Impact**
- Assumptions and numbers
- Mechanisms of change (cause → effect)
- Acquisition, monetization, strategic fit
- International relevance or constraints

**Substantiation**
Use at least one:
- **User data points:** interviews, feedback, observed behavior
- **Market research:** trends, competitors, benchmarks
- **Logic & intuition:** grounded reasoning with clear justification

**Early Validation (Fastest Tests Before Building)**
Goal: prove assumptions wrong as quickly as possible.
What's the cheapest, easiest experiment? Examples: analyze existing user data, fake-door test, survey card, prototype test, manual concierge, A/B copy test.

## When to use this vs. the [Hypothesis Template](./hypothesis-template.md)

**Idea Template** = upstream. Use when an idea enters the IDEA Board. Captures *what* and *why it matters*. Progresses through phases as the idea matures.

**Hypothesis Template** = downstream. Use when an idea moves toward Go/No-Go. Captures the *testable prediction* and *kill criteria*.

An idea can exist without a hypothesis. A hypothesis should not exist without a well-formed idea.
