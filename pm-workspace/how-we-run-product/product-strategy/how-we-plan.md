# How We Plan

## Weekly Product Cadence

**When:** Thursday, 30 min

This meeting covers three things: what we learned, where we're going, and what's blocking us.

### Agenda

**1. Learning (10 min)**
- Evaluate shipped features ([Week 1/2 checks](../shipping-and-release/))
- New insights: product, user feedback, research, market

**2. Roadmap Update (15 min)**
- Look at next 6-ish weeks
- Prioritize new incoming ideas (using [Explore/Exploit](./how-we-make-decisions.md) + [RISCE](./how-we-make-decisions.md#risce-scoring))

**3. Delivery Excellence (5 min)**
- Release deadlines
- Impediments

### Prep by Role

| Role | Brings to meeting |
|---|---|
| **Merlijn (Product)** | Roadmap update, strategic context, user support input, Go/No-Go decisions |
| **Niek (Growth)** | Growth metrics, North Star progress, analytics insights |
| **Ilayda (Design/Research)** | User research insights (2-3 findings), design feasibility, usability results |
| **Iman (Dev)** | Capacity assessment, technical risks, feasibility flags |

## Roadmap Rhythm

| Horizon | What | How often reviewed |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** (this week) | Active work with clear owners | Daily standups |
| **Next** (2-6 weeks) | Prioritized, discovery complete or underway | Weekly cadence meeting |
| **Later** (6+ weeks) | Parked ideas, lower priority | Monthly or when capacity frees up |
| **Unsure** | Needs signal or strategic clarity | Reviewed in periodic review |

## Quarterly Check

Every quarter, step back and ask:
1. Is our North Star still the right metric?
2. Are we in the right Explore/Exploit balance?
3. What did we kill, and what did we learn from it?
4. Which bets paid off? Which didn't? Why?

---

*Planning is about making choices, not making lists.*
