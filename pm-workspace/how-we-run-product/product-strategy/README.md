# Product Strategy

> What do we build, and why?

## North Star Metric

> *"The best product teams I've worked with have a single metric that everyone can recite. When you're deciding between two features, the North Star is the tiebreaker."*
> — **Lenny Rachitsky**

**Primary Metric:** Monthly Active Users (MAU)

**Pillar Metrics** (leading indicators):

| Pillar | Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Clarify** | [e.g., Summaries viewed per user] | Understanding leads to confidence |
| **Connection** | [e.g., Care Circle invites sent] | Support network activation |
| **Convenience** | [e.g., Appointments prepared] | Reduces friction in care journey |

**Every feature must connect:**
- **Direct impact:** Moves the North Star measurably
- **Indirect impact:** Improves retention/activation that feeds the North Star
- **No impact:** Why are we building this?

## IDEA Board

**Location:** [Jira Product Discovery - BIGB](https://dittocare.atlassian.net/jira/polaris/projects/BIGB/ideas/view/5417433)

### Adding Ideas

Everyone can add ideas. Requirements:

1. No duplicates
2. Self-explanatory title and description
3. Add **Insights** on why this idea is on the list
4. Add **Area of Improvement** (how it makes Ditto more valuable)
5. Best guess on **Impact and Effort**
6. Use the [Idea Template](../discovery/idea-template.md)

### Statuses

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Parking Lot** | Not currently working on |
| **Discovery** | Gathering data points, validation, mockups |
| **Ready for Delivery** | Clear spec, passed Go/No-Go, budget + duration estimate |
| **In Progress** | In development (must have Jira Epic attached) |

### Roadmap Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Now** | People are working on it this week |
| **Next** | Prioritized, discovery complete or in progress |
| **Later** | Lower priority, not yet in discovery |
| **Unsure** | Needs more clarity or signal |

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why it's dangerous | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **No success hypothesis** | Can't measure success if you didn't define it | Require [hypothesis](../discovery/hypothesis-template.md) before Go/No-Go |
| **Skipping discovery for Explore bets** | Big bets need big evidence | Enforce evidence bar |
| **"Users will love it"** | Confidence without mechanism | Require the "because" in hypothesis |
| **Discovery stops at Build** | Assumptions change, users surprise you | Weekly user touchpoints during Build |
| **Ignoring misses** | Failed features are gold if you learn | Require Learning Log entry |
| **0% kill rate** | Means you're not taking real bets | Target 10-20% kill rate in discovery |
