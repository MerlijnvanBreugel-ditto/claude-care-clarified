## Direction Analysis

### Direction A: On-Brand
**Best For:** Users who prefer familiar button-based interfaces
**Trade-offs:**
- ✅ Clear, fast to use
- ✅ No learning curve
- ❌ Feels like a form
- ❌ No emotional depth

### Direction B: Social
**Best For:** Younger users, social media natives
**Trade-offs:**
- ✅ Engaging, story-like
- ✅ Support history visible
- ❌ Could feel performative
- ❌ Streak pressure concerns

### Direction C: Mindful (Recommended)
**Best For:** All users, especially chronic patients
**Trade-offs:**
- ✅ Ritual > transaction
- ✅ Calms anxiety
- ✅ Creates connection
- ⚠️ Requires onboarding
- ⚠️ Unfamiliar paradigm

---

## Our Recommendation: Direction C (Mindful)

### Why Direction C

1. **User Research Alignment**: Users want low-friction, meaningful connection
2. **Loved Ones Multiplier**: Both sides of the exchange get beautiful experience
3. **Panel Consensus**: 5/5 experts approved after Round 2
4. **Emotional Language**: "Peaceful/Steady/Need comfort" > "Good/Okay/Bad"
5. **Consistency**: Matches Voice Check-in and Medication Reminders aesthetic

### Key Screens

| Screen | Primary User | Purpose |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| Morning Check-in | Patient | Share daily status via orb selection |
| Pulse Feed | Caregiver | View loved one's status, send support |
| Gesture Picker | Caregiver | Choose support gesture to send |
