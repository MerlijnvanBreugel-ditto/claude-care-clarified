# Kanker.nl Appstore: Competitive Analysis & Opportunities for Ditto

> Linear: [PAR-242](https://linear.app/ditto-care/issue/PAR-242) | Date: 2026-06-10 | Author: Mewtwo (research run: 105 verification agents + direct crawl of all 50 listings + NL App Store / Play Store data, all verified live 2026-06-10)

## Verdict in four lines

1. **There is no head-on competitor for Ditto's core anywhere in the catalog.** Nothing else combines AI conversation summaries, appointment prep, and care-circle sharing. The closest feature overlaps are OWise (raw audio recording, no AI, 9 NL App Store ratings in 13 years) and Stamps (care-circle updates, but the patient writes everything by hand).
2. **Ditto is already the most-rated app in the entire store.** 264 NL App Store ratings at 4.61★ and 50K+ Play installs. That is more iOS ratings than all other standalone apps in the catalog combined (~139). Ditto was listed June 4, 2026 with "Goed" on all four review dimensions.
3. **The "most are poor quality" hypothesis is confirmed, with two real exceptions.** Kanker.nl's own conversation app is abandoned (last update Oct 2020). OWise is buggy and barely used. ~40 of the 50 items are white-label mental-health modules from three GGZ platforms. The exceptions: Untire (RCT evidence, actively maintained, but tiny base and an engagement problem) and Stamps (small but genuinely loved).
4. **The channel question is not "get listed", it's "get featured".** Ditto is in. The play is editorial cross-links from kanker.nl's high-traffic condition pages, succession of their dead Gesprekshulp app, and using the IKNL/GGD vetting as a trust badge everywhere.

## The channel: what kanker.nl's Appstore actually is

The "Onco Appstore" launched February 2021 as a joint initiative of IKNL, NFK, Borstkankervereniging Nederland, KWF and Stichting kanker.nl. Every listed tool is tested by IKNL and GGD (GGD appstore testmethode) on usability, reliability and privacy, and must be scientifically researched, guideline-based, or from a trusted organization. The Ministry of Health's Zorg van Nu platform points patients and professionals to it as *the* overview of tested cancer apps. (All verified 3-0 by adversarial check.)

What this means for Ditto:
- **Trust badge, durably earned.** "Getoetst door IKNL en GGD" is third-party validation we can quote in onboarding, App Store copy, and B2B decks (Ikazia, AYA, Menzis). Few competitors of any kind carry it.
- **Unknown referral volume.** Presence is proven; installs driven by the listing are not measured. Attribution from the kanker.nl listing links is currently invisible to us (no UTM on the store links; ties into the broader Adjust/Mixpanel attribution mess).
- **Some users get apps reimbursed via kanker.nl "tegoed"** (credit). Untire reviews explicitly mention the first year paid through kanker.nl. Ditto is free, so the tegoed doesn't matter for us, but it shows kanker.nl actively funds usage of listed apps.

## Success cases: the hypothesis check

You expected most to be poor quality. Mostly right, and the verified evidence sharpens it:

- **Untire is the category's best case and still illustrates the trap.** Peer-reviewed RCT (799 participants, Psycho-Oncology 2020): fatigue improved, effect sizes d=0.35-0.40. But only 31% of the intervention arm completed the 12-week assessment (authors blame the "impersonal character" of app-only interventions), the original app was fully delisted, and after a 2024 relaunch it has 95 NL ratings. Evidence-rich, engagement-poor.
- **Oncokompas (the institutional darling, web tool) failed its primary endpoint** in a 625-patient RCT across 14 Dutch hospitals (Lancet Oncology 2020): no significant effect on patient activation, negligible QALY difference. Institutional blessing does not produce engagement.
- **Category-level quality is mediocre**: mean MARS quality 2.98/5 across 46 oncology apps (JMIR 2020); only 11% scored "good".
- **The demand is real and unconverted**: in NFK's national survey (n=4,196 Dutch cancer patients, 2023), 70% were willing to use a health app, only 42% actually did. The authors point at missing clinician encouragement and clinical integration. That 28-point gap is the market.

So: the catalog's curation filters out junk, but what survives is mostly low-traction or effectiveness-challenged. The two genuine successes (Untire, Stamps) both prove a piece of Ditto's thesis: validated content alone doesn't retain (Untire), and care-circle sharing is loved but shouldn't require the patient to do the writing (Stamps).

## Gap analysis: unmet needs in the catalog

1. **Understanding the conversation itself.** OWise records raw audio. Gesprekshulp records and is dead. Nobody summarizes, translates jargon, or extracts action points. This is Ditto's core and it is unowned.
2. **Appointment preparation.** Only static question lists (Gesprekshulp, Bbewust, breast-cancer-only). No personalized, context-aware prep.
3. **Effortless care-circle informing.** Stamps proves the need and the love, but the sick person must write every update. Highest-friction moment, manual solution.
4. **Sustained engagement across the journey.** Everything in the catalog is a single-niche episode (a fatigue course, a grief module, an end-of-life checklist). Nothing accompanies the patient longitudinally. Untire's 31% trial retention shows content-course retention is structurally weak.
5. **The caregiver as a user.** A third of the catalog targets naasten, all of it as "manage your own stress" courses. Nothing gives caregivers visibility into the actual care (that's Pal's ambition, at 1K installs).

## Top 5 opportunities for Ditto

1. **Become the successor to kanker.nl's own dead Gesprekshulp app.** Their app does question lists + recording, hasn't been touched since Oct 2020, and Ditto is the modern version of exactly that job. Propose kanker.nl retire/redirect it to Ditto. Worst case they say no and we've reminded the gatekeepers (IKNL/NFK) what Ditto does. Next step: Bart/Hester raise it with the appstore contact (appstore@kanker.nl) after we've optimized our listing.
2. **Get featured beyond the appstore listing.** Kanker.nl's condition and "net gediagnosticeerd" pages carry the real traffic; the appstore is a side door. Editorial cross-links ("hoe bereid ik me voor op het gesprek met mijn arts" → Ditto) are the volume play. Also: their listing shows our WhatsApp support and info@dittocare.com; add UTM-tagged store links if they allow it, so we can finally see channel volume.
3. **Use "getoetst door IKNL en GGD" as a trust badge.** In-app onboarding, store listings, B2B decks, AYA/Ikazia conversations. The NFK survey says clinician/institutional endorsement is what converts willing patients (70%) into users (42%). We have the endorsement; we barely use it.
4. **Partner, don't compete, with the niche winners.** Untire (fatigue) and Healthy Chronos (energy diary) solve the #1 post-treatment complaint that will surface constantly in Ditto summaries. A "your summary mentions fatigue → Untire is vetted, first year via kanker.nl" referral is a cheap, concrete first proof of the care-intelligence layer. Stamps overlaps more directly; watch rather than partner.
5. **Take OWise's job in oncology.** Their feature set (treatment tracking, side-effect graphs, recordings) validated demand 13 years ago; their execution (login bugs, raw audio, 9 ratings) never converted it. "Recording is not understanding" is the positioning line. Their side-effect trend graphs are the one feature worth studying for the oncology path.

## Recommended positioning angle on kanker.nl

**"De gratis app die elk gesprek met je arts omzet in heldere taal, en je dierbaren moeiteloos op de hoogte houdt."**

Positioning logic: every other tool in the catalog asks the patient to *do work* (follow a course, write updates, fill in diaries). Ditto is the only one that takes work away. Complement, don't attack, the psychosocial modules: Ditto handles the medical conversation layer; their courses handle coping. That framing also keeps the gatekeepers (IKNL/NFK, who built the dead Gesprekshulp and curate the store) as allies rather than threatened incumbents.

## Caveats & open questions

- Referral/install volume from kanker.nl is unmeasured. Listing value as a distribution lever vs. trust badge is unquantified until we tag links or ask kanker.nl for click data.
- App Store rating counts undercount hospital/association-distributed usage (OWise especially is pushed via Borstkankervereniging; Stamps via Olijf/Longkanker Nederland).
- Re-vetting cadence of the IKNL/GGD screen is undocumented; our "Goed" rating's maintenance requirements are unknown. Worth asking when we contact them.
- The NFK adoption survey self-selects digitally engaged patients; real-world adoption is likely at or below 42%, which widens the gap, not narrows it.
