## The one-paragraph takeaway

The same five problems recur across every life-changing diagnosis: people don't understand or remember their care, no one sees the whole picture over time, loved ones are locked out, the daily work of symptoms/meds/mood is unsupported, and the emotional load is carried alone. ditto's appointment summary attacks one episodic slice. The category-defining flip is to change the unit **from appointment → day** and **from patient → patient-and-circle** — a *Living Health Companion* where the summary becomes the periodic crystallization of a daily, multi-player relationship. The vision is bold; the build should be narrow and evidence-gated, because **monetization, daily engagement, and network effects are unproven hypotheses, not wins** — and the cheapest next step is to validate the frequency problem against ditto's own usage data.

## Method & limitations
External/public sources only (no internal ditto data this round). Pan-EU, anchored NL + DACH. Tiered sourcing with confidence + EU-relevance flags; three anchor claims independently re-verified. See [`sources.md`](sources.md) for caveats (incl. HTTP-403 fetch fallbacks and US-derived flags).
