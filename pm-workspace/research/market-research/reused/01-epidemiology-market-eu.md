# Epidemiology & Addressable-Market Sizing in Europe — Four "Life-Changing Diagnosis" Groups

**Prepared for:** ditto.care (patient + caregiver software companion)
**Geography:** Pan-EU, anchored on Netherlands (NL) + DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)
**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Author:** Market & epidemiology research

> **Source tiers:** Tier 1 = peer-reviewed / EUROSTAT / OECD / ECIS-IARC / national bodies (RIVM, Nivel, RKI). Tier 2 = patient orgs / professional societies. Tier 3 = news / company (texture only, never the sole basis for a number).
> **Note on method:** Several primary publisher sites (OECD, Alzheimer Europe, EurekAlert, RKI, Parkinson's Europe) blocked direct page-fetching during this research. Numbers below were extracted via search-engine reading of those same pages plus peer-reviewed mirrors (PMC, Lancet, ScienceDirect). Where a figure rests only on a search-snippet of a primary page, confidence is capped at Medium and flagged.

---

## Key takeaways

- **Cancer is the largest pool by far:** ~**2.8 million new cancer diagnoses/year** in the EU and **~22.3 million people alive in the EU-27 who have ever had a cancer diagnosis** (EUROCARE-6, 2020 data). [ECIS/ENCR, 2022](https://www.encr.eu/news/2022-cancer-incidence-and-mortality-estimates); [EUROCARE-6 / Lancet Oncology, 2024](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(23)00646-0/abstract) — **Confidence: High**
- **Dementia is the largest neurodegenerative pool:** ~**9.07 million people with dementia in the EU-27 (2025)**, rising ~58% by 2050. [Alzheimer Europe, 2025](https://www.alzheimer-europe.org/resources/publications/prevalence-dementia-europe-2025) — **Confidence: High**
- **Stroke is the largest acute pool:** ~**1.12 million incident strokes/year in the EU and ~9.5 million stroke survivors** (2017 GBD figures). [GBD / Stroke (AHA), 2020](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/STROKEAHA.120.029606) — **Confidence: High**
- **Autoimmune cluster is sizeable and chronic-young:** IBD ~**2.6–3 million** prevalent in Europe; MS ~**1.1 million** in the WHO Europe region; SLE/lupus ~**150,000–270,000** in the EU; type-1 diabetes affects hundreds of thousands (Germany alone ~341,000 adults). [Oxford/ECCO-JCC, 2024](https://academic.oup.com/ecco-jcc/article/18/Supplement_2/ii3/7848697); [Atlas of MS, 2020](https://www.msif.org/news/2023/08/21/new-prevalence-and-incidence-data-now-available-in-the-atlas-of-ms/); [SLEuro / Lancet Rheum modelling, 2023](https://sleuro.org/global-epidemiology-of-systemic-lupus-erythematosus-a-comprehensive-systematic-analysis-and-modelling-study/) — **Confidence: Medium-High**
- **Caregiver multiplier is large:** ~**100 million informal carers in the EU** overall; ~1-in-3 adults provide some informal care; **80% of all long-term care in Europe is delivered by informal carers**. A conservative per-patient multiplier of **1.3–2 closely involved loved ones** is well supported. [Eurocarers](https://eurocarers.org/about-carers/) — **Confidence: Medium**
- **NL anchor numbers:** ~**290,000** people with dementia in NL (Alzheimer Nederland; RIVM baseline 261,000 in 2022), and NL cancer incidence runs **above** EU average (710 vs 684 per 100k men; 589 vs 488 women, age-standardised). [Alzheimer Nederland via NL Times, 2024](https://nltimes.nl/2024/11/11/number-dutch-dementia-increase-66-2040); [OECD Country Cancer Profile NL, 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/02/eu-country-cancer-profile-netherlands-2025_de5a7cf3/69517d2d-en.pdf) — **Confidence: Medium-High**
- **Headline addressable population (EU, patients only):** roughly **34–38 million people** currently living with one of the four diagnosis groups; adding loved ones at a 1.3–2x multiplier yields a **patient + caregiver pool of ~55–95 million** (model and assumptions below). — **Confidence: Medium**
- **Annual "newly-diagnosed" inflow** (the prime acquisition moment for a diagnosis companion) is on the order of **4–5 million people/year in the EU** across the four groups combined. — **Confidence: Medium**

| Prevalence growth | +41% 2010→2020 (16.8M→23.7M), ~3.5%/yr | Europe | [EUROCARE-6, 2024](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(23)00646-0/abstract) (T1) | High |
| Germany new cases/yr | **~493,000** (≈262k men, 231k women) | DE | [RKI "Cancer in Germany" via krebsdaten.de](https://www.krebsdaten.de/Krebs/EN/Content/Publications/Cancer_in_Germany/cancer_in_germany_node.html) (T1) | Medium-High |
| Germany lifetime risk | 49% men, 43% women will be diagnosed | DE | [RKI](https://www.rki.de/EN/Topics/Noncommunicable-diseases/Physical-health/Cancer/cancer-node.html) (T1) | High |
| Netherlands incidence | 710/100k men, 589/100k women (above EU avg) | NL | [OECD Country Cancer Profile NL, 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/02/eu-country-cancer-profile-netherlands-2025_de5a7cf3/69517d2d-en.pdf) (T1) | Medium-High |

**Age profile & trajectory:** Strongly age-weighted (majority of diagnoses age 60+), but with a notable and rising early-onset segment (e.g. colorectal cancer rising in young Dutch adults). Trajectory is mixed: acute crisis at diagnosis → intensive treatment phase → either chronic survivorship (the 14.85M >5-yr survivors) or palliative/end-of-life. The diagnosis moment and the treatment-navigation phase are the highest-intensity windows for a companion product.

**Most common types:** Men — prostate (23%), lung & colorectal (14% each). Women — breast (~30%), colorectal (12%), lung (9%). [ECIS, 2022](https://www.encr.eu/news/2022-cancer-incidence-and-mortality-estimates)

**Caregiver involvement:** ~65% of cancer patients have a single primary caregiver, but a meaningful share have 1–3 involved (questionnaire studies routinely capture 1.4 caregivers/patient). Cancer caregivers provide ~33 hrs/week. [Informal Caregiving for Cancer Patients, PMC 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3816360/) (T1) — **Confidence: Medium**. *US data — EU applicability uncertain* for the "4 million / 53 million caregivers" headline.

**Age & trajectory:** Overwhelmingly age 65+; chronic-progressive over years. Caregiving burden is among the highest of any condition and is the defining feature — the "patient" is often non-autonomous, so the loved one is effectively the primary product user. This makes dementia a caregiver-first segment.

Central estimate **1.6x**.

## Addressable population model

**Goal:** top-down estimate of the EU population a patient + caregiver software companion could serve.

### Step 1 — EU prevalent "people living with it" pool (patients only)

| Group | EU prevalent patients (central estimate) | Basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cancer (ever-diagnosed, alive) | 22.3 million | EUROCARE-6 EU-27, 2020 | High |
| Dementia | 9.1 million | Alzheimer Europe EU-27, 2025 | High |
| Parkinson's | 0.8 million | derived (~175/100k × 448M) | Medium |
| ALS | 0.04 million | derived (~9.6/100k) | Medium |
| Stroke survivors | 9.5 million | GBD EU, 2017 | High |
| AMI / cardiac survivors | ~3–4 million | derived from annual AMI × survival (illustrative) | Low |
| MS | 0.6 million | derived (133/100k EU-27) | Medium |
| SLE / lupus | 0.2 million | SLEuro EU estimate | Medium |
| Type-1 diabetes | ~2.5–3 million | EU extrapolation from DE prevalence | Medium |
| IBD | ~2.5–3 million | EU extrapolation, ECCO-JCC | Medium-High |
| **Subtotal (with overlap caveat)** | **~50 million raw → ~34–38 million de-duplicated** | see assumptions | Medium |

> **Overlap & double-count caveat:** Cancer + stroke + cardiac pools overlap (comorbidity in older patients), and "survivor" pools include long-stable cases who are no longer in an active life-changing moment. We discount the raw ~50M sum by ~25–30% to ~**34–38 million** to reflect overlap and to exclude the long-tail of stable/low-engagement survivors.

### Step 2 — Annual newly-diagnosed inflow (the prime acquisition moment)

| Group | EU new cases / year | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cancer | ~2.8 million | High |
| Stroke | ~1.12 million | High |
| AMI (hospitalisations) | ~0.75–0.8 million | Medium |
| Dementia (incidence) | ~estimated 1.0–1.5 million* | Low |
| Parkinson's | ~70,000–85,000 | Medium |
| ALS | ~10,000–12,000 | Medium |
| MS | ~30,000–50,000 | Medium |
| Type-1 diabetes | tens of thousands | Medium |
| IBD | ~50,000–150,000 | Medium |
| **Combined annual inflow** | **~4–5 million people/year** | Medium |

*Dementia incidence is not directly sourced here and is inferred from prevalence/turnover — **flagged Low confidence**, verify against a dedicated incidence source.

### Step 3 — Apply caregiver multiplier

| Scenario | Patients | × multiplier | Patient + caregiver pool |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 34M | 1.3 | **~44 million** |
| Central | 36M | 1.6 | **~58 million** |
| Broad | 38M | 2.0 | **~76 million** |

> **Annual-cohort view (more relevant for a "diagnosis moment" companion):** ~4–5M new patients/year × 1.6 ≈ **~6.5–8 million new patient+caregiver dyads entering the funnel each year in the EU**.

### Assumptions table (explicit)

| # | Assumption | Value used | Rationale / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | EU-27 population | ~448 million | Eurostat order of magnitude |
| A2 | "Addressable" = currently living with one of the 4 groups | prevalent pools above | Excludes purely historical/cured-and-forgotten cases via the discount |
| A3 | Overlap/stable-case discount on raw prevalence sum | −25–30% | Comorbidity overlap + long-stable survivors disengaged; **largest uncertainty** |
| A4 | Caregiver multiplier | 1.3 / 1.6 / 2.0 | From Eurocarers structure + cancer-caregiver studies; condition-dependent |
| A5 | Parkinson's, ALS, MS, T1D, IBD, AMI-survivor EU totals | derived by rate × population | Not direct EU headcounts → Medium/Low confidence |
| A6 | Dementia incidence | inferred, not sourced | **Low confidence — verify** |
| A7 | Digital/smartphone reachability & willingness-to-use | NOT applied | This is a TAM, not SAM/SOM. Apply adoption %, device access, and reimbursement filters downstream to get a realistic serviceable market |

**Bottom line:** the EU TAM for a patient + caregiver diagnosis companion across these four groups is roughly **34–38 million patients / 44–76 million people including loved ones**, with a fresh **~4–5 million-patient annual inflow** representing the highest-intent acquisition moment. Cancer, dementia, and stroke alone account for the large majority of the pool.
