## The frame that wins

The v1 five-routes framing has been **superseded by v2's two-layer architecture**, and the deep dive confirms v2 is the better frame:

> **One spine (intelligence on the understood conversation, with the record as multiplier and a human/care-circle as the responder that delivers advising value below the device line), two frequency engines (treatment-window tracking, the reactive narrative loop), two leans (a light circle-share loop, the social surface on the care-graph), and everything operational and data-rail integrated, not owned. Monetised B2B, led by home-market hospital/insurer licensing.**

A+C from v1 survive as the customer and window choices inside that spine. B, D, E resolve into a capability, an integration, and a lean respectively. That is the reconciliation: the deep dive does not pick a new route, it collapses the five into one layered bet and tells you which parts are spine, which are engines, which are leans, and which are plumbing.
