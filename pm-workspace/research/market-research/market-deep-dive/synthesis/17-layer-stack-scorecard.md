## The recommended stack

> **Spine:** intelligence on the understood conversation, climbing from understanding to individualised advising **through a human / care-circle responder** (the only route that delivers Rung-2 value without becoming a Class IIa device), with **PHR integration as the multiplier** that gives the assistant the full picture.
> **Frequency engines:** active-treatment symptom tracking (F1) and the reactive narrative/guestbook loop (F2), both feeding the spine. These solve Ditto's episodic-frequency wound.
> **Leans:** a light "share my summary + next steps with my circle" coordination loop (F5) and the social surface on the care-graph (F7), neither a revenue line.
> **Integrate, do not own:** the operational front door (F8) and the data rails (MedMij / EHDS, F3/F4).
> **Monetise:** lead with **home-market B2B licensing** (hospital / insurer); hold **pharma-RWD as a consent-gated fork**; patient-pay is a topper; reimbursement is a deliberate, dated bet.

This is v2's two-layer architecture, validated and made concrete: own the intelligence, integrate the rails, treat the record as a multiplier, keep coordination and social as leans, and accept that the payer is an institution while the user is the patient.

## Open forks to carry into the strategy session

1. **Caregiver vs patient** as the primary customer (still open from the committed one-pager; three layers quietly assume the caregiver).
2. **Which B2B patron leads**, and **is pharma-RWD acceptable** given the trust wedge (the consent-first line).
3. **The NL operational front-door fork** (F8/C3): Doctolib is weak in NL; taking the booking seat means becoming a provider-SaaS company. A board decision, not a product call.
4. **How far up the intelligence ladder** before Ditto chooses to become a regulated device (the Rung-2 line; the human-in-loop buys time, not exemption).
5. **The pending circle metrics** (`16`/`C5`) that gate the social bet: retention by circle size is the one that settles whether the family loop is a growth engine or a vanity feature.

These are decisions for Merlijn and the team, not gaps in the research. The research has done its job when these are the questions left.
