# YC Spring 2026 — B2C Patient-Facing Agents

Competitive scan of the patient-facing healthcare startups in Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch (internal code **P2026**, Demo Day 16 Jun 2026, ~196 companies). Triggered by a LinkedIn "Healthcare Startups – Positioning Map" (creator handle "saulgh"), which plotted ~16 YC P2026 health companies on two axes: B2B↔B2C and AI autonomy.

We researched the five companies the map placed in the **top-right "B2C Autonomous Agents" quadrant** — Ditto's sphere — plus a lighter pass on the rest of the map and the batch/source itself.

## Files

1. **`b2c-quadrant-deep-dive.md`** — the payload. Five-company teardown (Juno, Clara, Framewise Health, Lumius, Adialante), each on what-they-do / model / geography / relevance-to-Ditto, plus the verdict for Ditto.
2. **`full-map-and-source.md`** — batch + source verification, and one-line IDs of the other 11 (B2B) companies on the map.
3. **`sources.md`** — every source URL, by company.

## Headline finding

The map is **one analyst's editorial framing, not an official YC artifact**, and its quadrant placements are unreliable: **only 2 of the 5 "B2C agents" are genuinely consumer-facing** (Juno, Clara). Two are B2B medical *hardware* (Lumius 3D ultrasound, Adialante portable MRI) and one is a B2B-sold patient-education tool (Framewise). None is a *fully* autonomous agent — every patient-acting one keeps a clinician in the loop.

**The one that matters: Juno.** A UK B2C AI companion for chronic illness, ~125K patients and ~$85K MRR in roughly six months, turning daily symptom check-ins into a doctor-visit PDF and monetizing the data layer to pharma/providers. It is the closest analogue to Ditto's space and the clearest signal of patient pull, on a B2C-acquired path Ditto deliberately does not take.
