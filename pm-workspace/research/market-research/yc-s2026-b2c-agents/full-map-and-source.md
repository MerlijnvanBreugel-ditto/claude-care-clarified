# Full Map & Source Verification — YC Spring 2026 (P2026)

## Batch & source (verify before citing externally)

- **Batch is real.** YC ran a Spring 2026 batch; internal code **P2026** ("P" = Primavera/Spring). Demo Day wrapped **16 Jun 2026** with ~196 startups. Some third-party trackers loosely call it "S26" — treat that label as imprecise; YC profiles show P2026. *(FACT, sourced.)*
- **Healthcare is a meaningful slice** of the batch; no precise published count. All ~16 companies on the map are real P2026 profiles. *(INFERENCE / partial FACT.)*
- **The positioning map is one analyst's interpretation, not an official YC artifact.** The creator handle "saulgh" could not be located or attributed to a real name; the original LinkedIn post was not found via search. YC does not publish quadrant maps. **Company existence and descriptions are verifiable; the quadrant placements are editorial and, as the B2C deep dive shows, unreliable** (two hardware companies were mis-plotted as B2C agents; FinalDose, a therapeutics company, sits oddly on a "B2B Augmentation Tools" axis). Use the map for orientation only.
