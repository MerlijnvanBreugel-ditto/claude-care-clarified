# Learning: Shipping & Release Brainstorm

**Date:** 2026-02-19
**Workflow:** Brainstorming → Shipping & Release design
**Verdict:** Perfect — no corrections needed

## What worked

1. **Iterating the Confluence template in real-time** — pushing to Confluence, getting feedback, adjusting immediately. Table format > subsections for decision-making templates (subsections become reading, tables force decisions).

2. **Pre-linking follow-up epics in the release evaluation table** — pulling existing Phase 2/v2 epics from Jira and linking them in the "Next step" column. Saves meeting time and connects evaluation to action.

3. **Explore/Exploit/Polish as a communication driver** — the classification doesn't just describe the feature, it determines the entire activation plan. This made the communication matrix design straightforward.

4. **Connecting workflows** — the shipping workflow feeds into the product strategy meeting workflow (shipped features auto-populate the evaluation table). Avoids information silos between processes.

## Reinforce

- When building meeting templates: optimize for scanning and decision-making, not reading. Tables > prose.
- When designing processes: connect the output of one workflow to the input of another. No dead ends.
- Feature areas can be both Explore and Exploit simultaneously — classify by specific feature, not umbrella.
